## What is the Cabinet of Digital Terms?

[[Surveillance Capitalism]], [[Dark Patterns|Dark Pattern]], [[Ghosting]], [[Red Pill]]?

You have heard these terms, and you probably use some of them. But do you know where they came from, who introduced them and why, what they make visible and what they quietly leave out?

The **Cabinet of Digital Terms** is a ==Cabinet of Curiosities==[^curiosities] for the digital age: it gathers **260+ terms** from digital media, platform culture, politics and popular culture in **20 clusters**, with **800+ external sources**. Every entry shows where a word comes from, who benefits from it, what it hides, and which other terms it belongs with. The Cabinet does not pretend to look from nowhere. As Donna Haraway put it: "Feminist objectivity means quite simply situated knowledges."

> *"Sometimes my jaw drops. Sometimes I feel misled, amazed or appalled by the digital world. That is why I started Project Digital Alertness. Together with designers, artists, researchers and students, we make the friction of this world visible in something you can experience, so that you discover connections and choose your own way more consciously. I am critical of AI, although I use it. Call me a ==Termophile==[^termophile]."*
>
> **Marieke de Vogel**, initiator of Project Digital Alertness

## Clusters and their unexpected questions

A cluster groups terms that belong to the same situation: the same platforms, the same people, the same pressure. Each cluster asks one question about the digital world, and its terms help you answer it. A term lives in one cluster, and its links show where it reaches into others.

## How to use the Cabinet

Wander through the Cabinet and let serendipity lead you: start with a [cluster](#clusters-and-their-unexpected-questions) that names something you already sense but cannot yet put into words, search for a term with the search bar, or rest the pointer on a circle in the map above to see how far a cluster reaches.

Or start with a question:

- *You meant to play for ten minutes and spend nothing. Who knew in advance where you would give in?* → [[Predatory Design]]
- *You watched her clean and felt calm. She was paid for it. How much were you paid?* → [[Cleanfluencer]]
- *You took off your shoes at the airport security check and felt safer. Were you?* → [[Security Theater]]

## Who it is for

- **Researchers:** the genealogy of terms, with the sources behind every entry.
- **Teachers and students:** a vocabulary for thinking critically about the platforms you use every day, in media, sociology, computer science and design.
- **Designers and developers:** how design steers behaviour, and where it is done differently.
- **Policymakers:** how algorithms and data shape society, and the rules that answer them.
- **Healthcare professionals:** the human side of digital health tools, AI and data.
- **Digital citizens and activists:** a name for an uncomfortable feeling online, and words to push back with.
