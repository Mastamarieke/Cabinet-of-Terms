## Opara-Martins et al. (2016) — Critical analysis of vendor lock-in and its impact on cloud computing migration: a business perspective

**Opara-Martins, J., Sahandi, R. & Tian, F. (2016). Critical analysis of vendor lock-in and its impact on cloud computing migration: a business perspective.** *Journal of Cloud Computing*. https://doi.org/10.1186/s13677-016-0054-z

---

### What this source contributes

A survey of 114 participants from UK businesses on vendor lock-in in cloud computing. Only 44% said they had a basic understanding of the term; lock-in risks grow as computing moves from on-premise to the cloud; and "the complexity and cost of switching providers is often under-appreciated until implementation." The authors trace lock-in to proprietary standards that block interoperability and portability, and propose strategies around contracts, standard formats and APIs.

### Analytical function in Vendor Lock-in

The structure of the lock, and why it appeals: the cost of leaving is invisible at the moment of choosing. It supports The Appeal and the landscape's wonder (the people inside the lock see it least).
