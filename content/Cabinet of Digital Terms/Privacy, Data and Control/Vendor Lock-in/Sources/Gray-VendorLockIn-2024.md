## Gray et al. (2024) — An Ontology of Dark Patterns Knowledge

**Gray, C. M., Santos, C., Bielova, N. & Mildner, T. (2024). An Ontology of Dark Patterns Knowledge: Foundations, Definitions, and a Pathway for Shared Knowledge-Building.** *CHI '24*. https://doi.org/10.1145/3613904.3642436

---

### What this source contributes

An ontology that brings together the dark-pattern typologies of researchers and regulators in three levels, with five high-level strategies: sneaking, obstruction, interface interference, forced action and social engineering. Under *Obstruction* sits the *Roach Motel*, which "subverts the user's expectation that an action will be as easy to reverse as it is to make, instead creating a situation that is easy to get into, but difficult to get out of", and *Immortal Accounts*, which make it "difficult or impossible to delete a user account once it has been created". Read for this entry in the arXiv version (2309.09640), whose title differs slightly from the published one.

### Analytical function in Vendor Lock-in

The design side of the lock: where leaving is made hard on purpose. It names what the Amazon example shows, and turns the curator's *opting out is possible on paper and nearly impossible in practice* into a recognised pattern.
