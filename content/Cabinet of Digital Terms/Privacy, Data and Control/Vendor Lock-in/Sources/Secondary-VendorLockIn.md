## 5. FTC Takes Action Against Amazon for Enrolling Consumers in Amazon Prime Without Consent and Sabotaging Their Attempts to Cancel

**Author:** Federal Trade Commission
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Government (regulator)
**Publisher:** FTC, 21 June
**URL:** https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/06/ftc-takes-action-against-amazon-enrolling-consumers-amazon-prime-without-consent-sabotaging-their

### What this source contributes

The FTC accused Amazon of using "manipulative, coercive, or deceptive user-interface designs known as 'dark patterns'" to enrol people in Prime and of making cancelling hard: users had to find the cancellation flow and click through several pages of offers. "Previous reporting about the process in the media has noted that Amazon used the term 'Iliad' to describe the process", after Homer's epic of the decade-long Trojan War. In September 2025 Amazon agreed to a $2.5 billion settlement.

## 6. De Correspondent en Follow the Money gaan samenwerken om onafhankelijker te worden van Amerikaanse big tech

**Author:** Rob Wijnberg and Sabine Bijleveld
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** De Correspondent, 19 February (members only)
**URL:** https://decorrespondent.nl/16752/de-correspondent-en-follow-the-money-gaan-samenwerken-om-onafhankelijker-te-worden-van-amerikaanse-big-tech

### What this source contributes

Two Dutch journalism platforms start a multi-year project to reduce their dependence on American tech companies. The announcement opens: "Dit artikel is geschreven op een laptop van Apple. De eindredactie vond plaats in een tekstverwerker van Google. De site waarop je dit leest, draait op servers van Amazon." The authors list the tools their work depends on (Slack, Mailchimp, Trello, Adobe, Stripe, Docusign) and say leaving is hard because American tech is "vaak superieur in gebruiksgemak, betaalbaar en soms zelfs de enige optie".
