## Zuboff (2015) — Big Other: Surveillance Capitalism and the Prospects of an Information Civilization

**Zuboff, S. (2015).** Big Other: Surveillance Capitalism and the Prospects of an Information Civilization. *Journal of Information Technology*. https://doi.org/10.1057/jit.2015.5

---

### What this source contributes

The first academic paper on surveillance capitalism, which it describes as an emerging logic of accumulation in the networked sphere. Zuboff reads Google's practices through two articles by its chief economist, Hal Varian, who names four uses of computer-mediated transactions: data extraction and analysis, new contracts made possible by better monitoring, personalisation and customisation, and continuous experiments. From these she draws the implicit logic of surveillance capitalism and the global architecture of computers it depends on.

That architecture produces a new expression of power, distributed and largely uncontested, which she names *Big Other*. It works through mechanisms of extraction, commodification and control that are often illegible, and that "effectively exile persons from their own behavior" while creating new markets in behavioural prediction and modification. She argues that surveillance capitalism challenges democratic norms and departs in key ways from the history of market capitalism.

### Analytical function in Surveillance Capitalism

The source answers the Origin question: who named this, and with which image. It dates the word to an academic paper four years before the book, shows that it was built on Google's own descriptions of what it does, and gives the image (*Big Other*) that places the term between Bentham's panopticon and Orwell's Big Brother.

### About the author(s)

Shoshana Zuboff is professor emerita at Harvard Business School.
