## 1. In the Age of the Smart Machine: The Future of Work and Power

**Author:** Shoshana Zuboff
**Year:** 1988
**Type:** Academic book
**Publisher:** Basic Books
**URL:** https://shoshanazuboff.com/book/books/in-the-age-of-the-smart-machine/

### What this source contributes

A study of information technology in the workplace. Among its concepts is the *information panopticon*: computer systems that make work visible to managers continuously, while workers cannot tell when they are being watched.

### Related entries

- [[Surveillance Capitalism]] — the older image of surveillance that Zuboff used before she named surveillance capitalism
