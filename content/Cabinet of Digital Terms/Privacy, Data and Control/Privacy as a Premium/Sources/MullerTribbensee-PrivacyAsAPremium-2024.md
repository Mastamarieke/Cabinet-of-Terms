## Müller-Tribbensee (2024) — Privacy Promise Vs. Tracking Reality in Pay-or-Tracking Walls

**Müller-Tribbensee, T. (2024).** Privacy Promise Vs. Tracking Reality in Pay-or-Tracking Walls. *Privacy Technologies and Policy: 12th Annual Privacy Forum, APF 2024*. Lecture Notes in Computer Science. Springer. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-68024-3_9

### What this source contributes

Pay-or-tracking walls, also called consent-or-pay models, cookie paywalls or pay-or-okay walls, ask users to pay a fee or consent to tracking. The study checks whether websites keep the privacy promise stated on their wall for those who pay. Across 341 websites, tracking is lower for paying users, but 32.9% of the websites fail to keep the promise; 80% of those could keep it by removing one or two trackers. Websites that share a joint subscription, with a provider that regularly checks for trackers, keep their promise better than others.
