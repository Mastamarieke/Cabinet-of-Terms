## Nouwens et al. (2020) — Dark Patterns after the GDPR: Scraping Consent Pop-ups and Demonstrating their Influence

**Nouwens, M., Liccardi, I., Veale, M., Karger, D. & Kagal, L. (2020).** Dark Patterns after the GDPR: Scraping Consent Pop-ups and Demonstrating their Influence. *Proceedings of the 2020 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems*. https://doi.org/10.1145/3313831.3376321

---

### What this source contributes

The authors scraped the designs of the five most popular consent management platforms on the top 10,000 websites in the UK and found that only 11.8% met minimal requirements based on European law. In a field experiment with 40 participants, removing the opt-out button from the first page increased consent by 22 to 23 percentage points, and more granular controls on the first page decreased consent by 8 to 20 percentage points.

### Analytical function in Privacy as a Premium

Shows the mechanism behind the choice: the screen that asks for consent is designed, and its design moves the answer. Refusing is not one click for everyone when the button to refuse is hidden.
