## Van Dijck (2014) — Datafication, dataism and dataveillance: Big Data between scientific paradigm and ideology

**Van Dijck, J. (2014).** Datafication, dataism and dataveillance: Big Data between scientific paradigm and ideology. *Surveillance & Society*, 12(2). https://doi.org/10.24908/ss.v12i2.4776

---

### What this source contributes

Metadata and data have become a currency with which people pay for communication services and security, a trade-off most people have grown comfortable with. Van Dijck takes apart the ideological grounds of datafication: it rests on problematic claims about what data is and what it can know, and it behaves like a widespread secular belief. That belief, dataism, succeeds because masses of people, naively or unwittingly, trust their personal information to corporate platforms, and extend the same faith to public institutions such as research and law enforcement that handle their data. The interlocking of government, business and academia in this ideology is why she asks for a critical look at the whole ecosystem of connective media.

### Analytical function in Dataism

It answers the Origin question of where the critical meaning of the word comes from: not Harari's description of a coming belief system, but an analysis of a belief already at work, held in place by trust.

### About the author(s)

José van Dijck was affiliated with the University of Amsterdam when the article appeared.
