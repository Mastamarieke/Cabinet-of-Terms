## Andrejevic & Selwyn (2020) — Facial recognition technology in schools: critical questions and concerns

**Andrejevic, M. & Selwyn, N. (2020). Facial recognition technology in schools: critical questions and concerns.** *Learning, Media and Technology*. https://doi.org/10.1080/17439884.2020.1686014

---

### What this source contributes

An examination of facial recognition and facial detection in schools, for campus security, automated registration and emotion detection, arguing that it is likely to alter schools "along divisive, authoritarian and oppressive lines". The authors note that the capacity of such systems to prevent school shootings "has been criticised as little more than 'security theatre', with the technology argued to 'offer only the appearance of safer schools'" (Andrew Ferguson, cited in Harwell 2018), and that the Swedish data protection authority began fining schools for facial recognition roll-call systems.
