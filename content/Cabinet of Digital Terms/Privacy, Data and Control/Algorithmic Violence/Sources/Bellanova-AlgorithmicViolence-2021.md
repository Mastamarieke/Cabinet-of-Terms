## Bellanova et al. (2021) — Toward a Critique of Algorithmic Violence

**Bellanova, R., Irion, K., Lindskov Jacobsen, K., Ragazzi, F., Saugmann, R. & Suchman, L. A. (2021).** Toward a Critique of Algorithmic Violence. *International Political Sociology*, 15(1). https://doi.org/10.1093/ips/olab003

---

### What this source contributes

A collective discussion that asks how algorithmic systems feed (into) specific forms of violence, and how they justify violent actions or redefine which forms of violence count as legitimate. It converges on three concerns: the violence that goes into creating and feeding data infrastructures; the translation at work when computer vision is used in security practices; and the institutional governing of algorithmic violence, its organisation, limitation and legitimation. The authors argue for situated analysis of algorithmic systems as part of complex and often messy practices.

### Analytical function in Algorithmic Violence

It answers the Origin question of what research did with the term: it moved from naming the harm to asking how it is made to look legitimate.
