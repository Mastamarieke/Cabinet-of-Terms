***The algorithm denied the benefit. No one decided. No one is responsible.***

**Literal meaning:** An *algorithm* is a fixed series of steps that solves a problem; the word comes from the name of the ninth-century Persian mathematician al-Khwārizmī. *Violence* usually means physical force that causes harm. **Algorithmic violence** joins the two: harm done by following the steps, without anyone raising a hand. It names the harm of automated decision-making systems, such as a benefit refused, ==a face misidentified==[^gaze] or a fraud check aimed at the wrong people.

[^gaze]: Joy Buolamwini, a researcher at MIT, found that the face-tracking software for her art project, the Aspire Mirror, did not see her dark face until she put on a white mask. She called it the *coded gaze*: systems that carry the biases of the people who build them. In 2018 her study with Timnit Gebru found error rates of up to 34.7 percent for darker-skinned women, against 0.8 percent for lighter-skinned men. [MIT News: 'Study finds gender and skin-type bias in commercial artificial-intelligence systems'](https://news.mit.edu/2018/study-finds-gender-skin-type-bias-artificial-intelligence-systems-0212) [Wikipedia: 'Joy Buolamwini'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joy_Buolamwini)

**Origin:** The artist ==Mimi Onuoha==[^onuoha] proposed the term in 2018, in an essay posted online. It names what happened as big data and AI were taken up by institutions: =="the subsequent incorporation and leveraging of all these things into a hierarchical and unequal society"== ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#1. Notes on Algorithmic Violence|Onuoha, 2018]]: Notes on Algorithmic Violence, on GitHub). She built it on *structural violence*, Johan Galtung's term of 1969 for harm done by social structures rather than by a person; Donna Haraway had already described old hierarchies carried on in information systems, the [[Informatics of Domination]]. Researchers then asked ==how such systems make violence look legitimate== ([[Bellanova-AlgorithmicViolence-2021|Bellanova et al., 2021]]: a collective discussion in international political sociology).

> Harm produced by automated systems whose design encodes existing inequalities — where no individual decides, and no individual is accountable.

**The Appeal:** Automated systems promise the same rules for everyone, with no tired official, no favourites and no bad day: a government can have millions of benefit applications checked the same way, and fast. A computer, the reasoning goes, cannot discriminate, the belief that [[Dataism]] names. Louder still, ==the new tech barons, Naomi Klein and Astra Taylor write, preach that only the AI "arms race" can save humanity== ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#12. End Times Fascism by Naomi Klein and Astra Taylor review – the fightback starts here|Trilling, 2026]]: review of their book End Times Fascism): [[e-acc (Effective Accelerationism)|e-acc]]. The same promise appeals to those who want to remove people at scale: in April 2025 the acting director of ==ICE==[^ice] said deportation should run [["Like Prime, but with human beings."|"like Prime, but with human beings"]] ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#10. ICE Head Wants Deportations to Run Like Amazon Prime: 'We Need to Treat This Like a Business'|Latin Times, 2025]]: Todd Lyons at the Border Security Expo in Phoenix). People handled as parcels: the everyday, bureaucratic form of [[Dehumanization]].

[^ice]: United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement, the federal agency that enforces immigration law. Its acting director from 2025 was Todd Lyons. [Wikipedia: 'U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Immigration_and_Customs_Enforcement)

**The Friction:** When the system is wrong, there is no one to hold to account. In the Netherlands it happened twice, to parents in the ==childcare benefits scandal==[^toeslagen] and to students at ==DUO==[^duo]: a risk model picked people out more often because of their nationality or migration background. A government had handed a decision to a calculation, [[Cybernetic Governance]], and the people it hit could not see why: a [[Black Box]]. The harm repeats because ==the system repeats== ([[Safransky-AlgorithmicViolence-2020|Safransky, 2020]]: the geographer calls algorithmic violence repetitive and standardised, and shows how an algorithm that tells US cities where to invest turns political choices into technical ones).

Governments also hand the calculation to companies. ==The Dutch police use software from Palantir, the company Peter Thiel co-founded; it was bought in secret, and of the documents released on request more than 99 percent was blacked out== ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#14. Minister: Nederlandse politie maakt gebruik van software Palantir|Security.NL, 2025]]: the justice minister's answers to parliamentary questions, August 2025): [[Sovereignty as a Service]]. Its co-founder ==Peter Thiel lectured in 2025 that the Antichrist would take over by talking about Armageddon "nonstop" and promising "peace and safety"== ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#16. Peter Thiel is delivering 4 private sold-out lectures at a club in San Francisco—about the Antichrist|Fortune, 2025]]: four off-the-record lectures in San Francisco, with fear of technology used to justify surveillance and control). The same company builds ImmigrationOS for ICE, for politicians who, in Klein and Taylor's words, ==turn visions of disaster into votes by promising "bunkered garrison states", with migration drawing most of the attention== ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#12. End Times Fascism by Naomi Klein and Astra Taylor review – the fightback starts here|Trilling, 2026]]: review of End Times Fascism). They call it [[Endtime Fascism|end times fascism]].

[^onuoha]: A Nigerian American artist and academic in New York whose work examines what data collection and technology do to society. [Wikipedia: 'Mimi Onuoha'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mimi_Onuoha) [Mimi Onuoha: website](https://www.mimionuoha.com)
[^toeslagen]: The Tax and Customs Administration's risk model made people without Dutch nationality more likely to be wrongly labelled as fraudsters. In 2021 Amnesty International called it a violation of human rights, and the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens fined the tax authority €2.75 million for its unlawful use of dual nationality. [[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#3. Amnesty ziet mensenrechtenschending bij toeslagenaffaire en vreest voor herhaling|NOS: 'Amnesty ziet mensenrechtenschending bij toeslagenaffaire en vreest voor herhaling']] [[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#5. Boete voor Belastingdienst van 2,7 miljoen voor discriminatie toeslagenouders|NOS: 'Boete voor Belastingdienst van 2,7 miljoen voor discriminatie toeslagenouders']]

[^duo]: From 2012 to 2023 DUO, which pays student finance, selected students with a migration background for fraud checks sooner than others. After reporting by NOS op 3 and Investico, an investigation found indirect discrimination, "unintended and unconscious", and in 2024 the government paid around ten thousand students back, €61 million in all. [[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#4. Geld terug voor studenten na discriminerende fraudeopsporing|NOS: 'Geld terug voor studenten na discriminerende fraudeopsporing']]

**Why This Matters:** Once you see **algorithmic violence**, a refused benefit or a fraud letter stops looking like bad luck and starts looking like a design. You meet it in the letter that says the system has selected you. What you can ask then is who made the rule, and how to reach a person who can change it. Makers can ask it before the system is built: [[Ethical Design]] treats dignity and autonomy as requirements from the start, and [[Design Justice]] puts the people a system is most likely to harm at the centre of designing it. Rules have started to follow, slowly: the EU's AI Act [classes systems that decide on public benefits as high-risk](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/ "Read Annex III of the AI Act, point 5(a)."), but ==in 2026 the EU postponed those rules to December 2027== ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#15. Digital Omnibus on AI|European Parliament, 2026]]: the Digital Omnibus on AI, after delays in standards and supervision and with an eye on competitiveness), and the [[Brussels Effect]] may carry them beyond Europe ([[Secondary-AlgorithmicViolence#8. The Brussels Effect and Artificial Intelligence|Siegmann & Anderljung, 2022]]: especially through large US technology companies).
