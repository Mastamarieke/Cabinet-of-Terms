## 4. S. 4711, statement of 7 December 2020 (Daniel Anderl Judicial Security and Privacy Act)

**Author:** U.S. Courts
**Year:** 2020
**Type:** Government statement
**Publisher:** U.S. Courts, 7 December
**URL:** https://www.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/statement_of_director_duff_dec_2020_final.pdf

### What this source contributes

In 2013 a gunman who targeted Chief Judge Timothy Corrigan had bought the judge's address on the internet for $1.95; the attacker of Judge Esther Salas found her home address online in 2020. The bill would make it unlawful for data brokers to sell judges' personal information.
