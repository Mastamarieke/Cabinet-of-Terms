## Crain (2018) — The limits of transparency: Data brokers and commodification

**Crain, M. (2018).** The limits of transparency: Data brokers and commodification. *New Media & Society*. https://doi.org/10.1177/1461444816657096

---

### What this source contributes

In the United States the main policy answer to the harms of internet surveillance is transparency. Crain argues that transparency, however laudable, runs into structural constraints within the political economy of commercial surveillance. A case study of the data broker industry shows that the commodification of personal information is at the root of the power imbalances that transparency-based consumer empowerment tries to correct, and that privacy policy needs a critical political economy of commercial surveillance.

### Analytical function in Data Brokers

It answers the Friction question of what holds the trade back, and why the obvious answer does not: telling people what brokers hold leaves the trade itself in place.
