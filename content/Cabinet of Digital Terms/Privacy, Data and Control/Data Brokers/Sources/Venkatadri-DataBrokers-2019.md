## Venkatadri et al. (2019) — Auditing Offline Data Brokers via Facebook's Advertising Platform

**Venkatadri, G., Sapiezynski, P., Redmiles, E. M., Mislove, A., Goga, O., Mazurek, M. & Gummadi, K. P. (2019).** Auditing Offline Data Brokers via Facebook's Advertising Platform. *The World Wide Web Conference*. https://doi.org/10.1145/3308558.3313666

---

### What this source contributes

Data brokers such as Acxiom and Experian collect and sell data on people, used for marketing and political campaigns, with very limited visibility into what they hold. Facebook's partnership with six data brokers across seven countries gave the authors a way in. A large share of Facebook accounts, above 90% in the US, were linked to data broker information. By running controlled ads to 183 US volunteers, they found that at least 40% of the data broker attributes were not at all accurate, that people differed widely in how much of their data was wrong, and that even financial information could be highly inaccurate.
