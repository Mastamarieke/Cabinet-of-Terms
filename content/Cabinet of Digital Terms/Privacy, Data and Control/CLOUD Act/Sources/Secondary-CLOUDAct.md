## 1. Clarifying Lawful Overseas Use of Data (CLOUD) Act

**Author:** United States Congress
**Year:** 2018
**Type:** Law
**Publisher:** US Congress, H.R. 4943 (115th Congress), March
**URL:** https://www.congress.gov/bill/115th-congress/house-bill/4943

### What this source contributes

The text of the law. It makes US providers disclose data "regardless of whether such communication, record, or other information is located within or outside of the United States", and it allows executive agreements with other countries.

## 4. FAQ avant de se lancer dans la qualification SecNumCloud

**Author:** ANSSI (Agence nationale de la sécurité des systèmes d'information)
**Year:** n.d.
**Type:** Government guidance
**Publisher:** ANSSI, France
**URL:** https://cyber.gouv.fr/enjeux-technologiques/cloud/faq-qualification-secnumcloud/

### What this source contributes

The French cybersecurity agency explains that SecNumCloud's protection against extraterritorial laws "repose sur l'assurance que votre société est soumise exclusivement aux lois de l'Union européenne": headquarters in an EU member state, non-EU shareholders in the minority, limits on non-EU third-party services, operational autonomy.
