## 8. Mozilla Study: Data Privacy Labels for Most Top Apps in Google Play Store are False or Misleading

**Author:** Mozilla Foundation
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Research report (consumer advocacy)
**Publisher:** Mozilla Foundation, 23 February
**URL:** https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/blog/mozilla-study-data-privacy-labels-for-most-top-apps-in-google-play-store-are-false-or-misleading/

### What this source contributes

Mozilla compared the Data Safety labels of the 40 most downloaded apps on Google Play with their own privacy policies and found discrepancies in nearly 80 percent. The labels of TikTok and Twitter said neither app shares personal data with third parties, while their privacy policies say they share it with advertisers, internet service providers and other companies. Google exempts data shared with *service providers* from the label.

## 9. Unpacking Privacy Labels: A Measurement and Developer Perspective on Google's Data Safety Section

**Author:** Rishabh Khandelwal et al.
**Year:** 2024
**Type:** Academic (peer-reviewed conference paper)
**Publisher:** 33rd USENIX Security Symposium
**URL:** https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity24/presentation/khandelwal

### What this source contributes

The first large-scale study of Google's Data Safety sections, across 1.1 million apps on Google Play: the labels contradict themselves, and developers both over- and under-report what they collect and share; interviews with developers show how hard the labels are to fill in. (Abstract read.)

## 10. Mark Zuckerberg: We do not sell data to advertisers

**Author:** TechCrunch
**Year:** 2018
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** TechCrunch, 10 April
**URL:** https://techcrunch.com/2018/04/10/mark-zuckerberg-we-do-not-sell-data-to-advertisers

### What this source contributes

In his Senate testimony of 10 April 2018, answering Senator John Cornyn, Mark Zuckerberg said: *"we do not sell data to advertisers."* The article explains why that is true in a narrow sense: advertisers tell Facebook whom they want to reach, for example women interested in skiing, and Facebook shows them the ads, so the data never change hands while Facebook still earns from them.
