***The end is coming. Only one group deserves to survive it.***

**Literal meaning:** **Endtime fascism** describes the political formation in which fascist or authoritarian politics are legitimised by apocalyptic urgency — the claim that the civilisational situation is so dire, the threat so existential, that the normal constraints of democratic governance, human rights, and legal process must be suspended. The emergency justifies the extraordinary.

**Origin:** The term was coined by Naomi Klein and Astra Taylor in a Guardian essay of 13 April 2025, *The rise of end times fascism*, and worked out in their book of September 2026. They name a formation whose members share the conviction that the common world is about to end, and who conclude from that conviction that only the chosen — chosen by wealth, by faith, or by ancestral claim to a piece of land — are to open the next phase of human history.

The older analytical framework comes from political theorists including Roger Griffin (fascism as palingenetic ultranationalism, the rebirth of the nation through catastrophe) and Robert Paxton (*The Anatomy of Fascism*, 2004). The eschatological dimension is documented across 20th century fascist movements and their contemporary successors. The digital amplification of apocalyptic political language has made the structure more visible and more rapidly distributable.

> Authoritarian politics that frames society as being in terminal decline — using fear of collapse to justify the removal of democratic constraints.

**The Appeal:** **Endtime fascism** speaks to genuine anxieties about civilisational change — immigration, demographic shift, economic disruption, technological transformation. These are real. The authoritarian response — strong leadership, clear enemies, urgent action — provides cognitive clarity and emotional relief in conditions of genuine complexity.

**The Friction:** [[Millenarianism]] is the ideological structure: corrupt present, catastrophic transition, chosen community. [[Armageddon Complex]] is the power-accumulation mechanism: the emergency justifies what would otherwise be illegitimate. [[Disaster Nationalism]] is the mainstream political form; **endtime fascism** is the more extreme endpoint. [[Technofascism]] is what **endtime fascism** looks like with advanced technology infrastructure. The specific harm of the "ordinary limits no longer apply" move is documented: what is suspended in emergency is rarely restored. The emergency that justified the suspension becomes the justification for its permanence.

Klein and Taylor argue that this is not the return of the fascism of the 1930s, and the difference is in what gets promised. Historical fascist movements imagined the destruction of particular parts of the world and offered their own working-class supporters something concrete on the far side of it: a plot of farmland taken from Slavs, a house taken from Jews, public works to connect the two. The leaders of end times fascism are too kleptocratic to deliver any of that, so what remains as the only binding principle is the apocalyptic expectation itself, which Umberto Eco in 1995 already listed as a permanent feature of fascism: since enemies have to be defeated, there must be a final battle, after which the movement will hold the world. The golden age is still promised at campaign rallies, [[Tradwife]] households and industrial jobs included, but nothing is being built to deliver it.

There is also a material difference. Mussolini and Hitler both died in April 1945, four months before the first atomic bombs. Neither knew a world with nuclear weapons, with measurable warming produced by ordinary economic activity, or with artificial intelligence. Apocalypse at planetary scale was not an instrument available to them.

The Cabinet's own subject appears in the delivery. At the World Economic Forum in Davos in January 2026, Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff presented a masterplan to rebuild Gaza as a resort city, illustrated with AI-generated images of towers beneath parting clouds. Leaked US military documents later showed that the housing planned for Palestinians would be reachable only through [[Biometric Surveillance]], with activities and purchases logged to datacenters nearby.

**Why This Matters:** **Endtime fascism** names the specific move of using apocalyptic urgency to suspend democratic constraint. Once you see the structure — situation too dire for ordinary limits — you can identify it across very different political contexts, and ask: what would it take for the emergency to be declared over, and who would make that declaration? Klein and Taylor end on the point that the cause is not the individuals who hold the power but the arrangements that allowed that much money, information technology and weaponry to collect in so few hands.
