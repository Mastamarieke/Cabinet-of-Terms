## Steinberg (2017) — Sharenting: Children's Privacy in the Age of Social Media

**Steinberg, S.B. (2017).** Sharenting: Children's Privacy in the Age of Social Media. *Emory Law Journal*, 66, 839. https://scholarship.law.ufl.edu/facultypub/779/

---

### What this source contributes

The foundational legal treatment of sharenting. Steinberg describes the online sharing of parenting and children, and observes that parents now shape their children's digital identity long before those children open their first email. She weighs the parent's interest in sharing against the child's interest in privacy, and argues that children have a claim to decide what is known about them.

### Analytical function in Sharenting

The source behind the Origin's account of how the word entered academic and legal debate, and behind the entry's definition: a digital identity built by a parent before the child can consent. Earlier versions attributed it to the wrong journal and to a statistic (1,000 images before age five) that is not in the article; both were corrected on 7 October 2026.
