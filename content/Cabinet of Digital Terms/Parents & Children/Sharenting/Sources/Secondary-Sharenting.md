## 3. ECLI:NL:RBGEL:2020:2521

**Author:** Rechtbank Gelderland
**Year:** 2020
**Type:** Court ruling
**Publisher:** Rechtspraak.nl
**URL:** https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/inziendocument?id=ECLI:NL:RBGEL:2020:2521

### What this source contributes

Summary proceedings of 13 May 2020: a grandmother is ordered to remove photos of her grandchildren from Facebook and Pinterest, and is barred from posting new ones, because under the GDPR children under sixteen need the consent of their legal representative; penalty of €50 a day up to €1,000.

## 4. ECLI:NL:GHARL:2020:5195

**Author:** Gerechtshof Arnhem-Leeuwarden
**Year:** 2020
**Type:** Court ruling
**Publisher:** Rechtspraak.nl
**URL:** https://uitspraken.rechtspraak.nl/inziendocument?id=ECLI:NL:GHARL:2020:5195

### What this source contributes

Decision of 7 July 2020: a mother may not post photos or videos of her six-year-old daughter on Facebook or Instagram for five years without the father's consent. The court cites the platform's terms, under which posting grants a worldwide, transferable licence, and the child's age, at which she cannot yet foresee the consequences.
