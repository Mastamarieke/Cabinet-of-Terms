## De Vettor et al. (2025) — Involuntary Celibacy (Incel) Identity: A Thematic Analysis of an Online Community's Beliefs and Emotional Experiences

**De Vettor, M., Lo Buglio, G., Barsanti, A., Ciocca, G., Gennaro, A., Goksal, R., Lingiardi, V., Giovanardi, G. & Boldrini, T. (2025).** Involuntary Celibacy (Incel) Identity: A Thematic Analysis of an Online Community's Beliefs and Emotional Experiences. *Societies*, 15(2), 44. https://doi.org/10.3390/soc15020044

### What this source contributes

A thematic analysis of one thread on an independent Italian incel forum: 453 contributions from 52 users, coded by two raters. The thread was started by a woman describing her own missed adolescence, which gave the study something most forum research lacks, a direct exchange between the community and an outsider who claims the same pain. Four themes emerged, named in the forum's own words: "non-persons are not like us" (114 occurrences), "I have no life" (52), "without experiences in adolescence you are ruined" (45), and "only ours is true suffering" (42).

The authors read the community's identity as built on gender role stress, specifically acceptance threat: the felt failure to meet the expectations of dominant masculinity. Their analytical move is to apply Halpin's *weaponized subordination* to the material. Incels place themselves at the bottom through the look-money-status theory, collectively accept that position as permanent, and then use it to justify misogyny: subordinate men employing dominant practices, with their own lowness as the licence. Suffering does double duty, as the thing the community shares and as the proof that outsiders cannot be taken seriously.

The study is the first on an independent forum rather than a moderated subreddit, and the first on an Italian community. Its limits are the ones the authors name: one thread, one country, and none of the silent members who read but never post.
