***He did not choose this identity. It chose him, gradually, through forums that explained his loneliness as injustice.***

**Literal meaning:** **Incel** — involuntary celibate — describes a person, typically a man, who cannot find a romantic or sexual partner despite wanting one, and who attributes this to factors beyond his control. The word now does two jobs. Inside the forums it is a name people give themselves, with a vocabulary and an explanation for why they are where they are. Outside them it is an insult that means loser, and each such use confirms what the forums already say: that the world holds them in contempt.

**Origin:** Coined by a Canadian woman named Alana in 1997 for her online support community, "Alana's Involuntary Celibacy Project", which was supportive and gender-mixed. After she moved on, other communities adopted the term and turned it into an ideology: rejection explained by female [[Hypergamy]] — the claim that women always trade up — and by fixed male hierarchies. The contemporary **incel** community is associated with several mass killings, beginning with Elliot Rodger in 2014.

> A term coined for a support community that was transformed into an ideology of resentment — where romantic failure becomes political identity.

**The Appeal:** The pain is real, and it has been measured. In the largest survey so far, of 272 self-identified incels, 64% showed depressive symptoms, 60% anxiety, 48% had thought about suicide, and one in four screened positive for autistic traits ([[Secondary-Incel|Speckhard et al. (2021)]]). The forums answer that: in the studies [[Sparks-Incel-2022|Sparks, Zidenberg and Olver (2022)]] review, 70% say the forums give them a sense of belonging and 75% feel understood there. Most people who take the name are looking for that, not for a political programme.

**The Friction:** The name is the problem, because of what it lets you do with it. [[SMV (Sexual Market Value)]] — attractiveness as a market hierarchy — puts incels at the bottom, and the market is rigged by **hypergamy** and male competition. [[Blackpill]] is the terminus: if the hierarchy is biological, rejection is permanent. What [[DeVettor-Incel-2025|De Vettor et al. (2025)]] found in 453 posts on an Italian forum is what happens next. The bottom position is not just accepted but claimed, and then used: because only their suffering counts, women can be dismissed as "non-persons", a word used 114 times in a single thread. Halpin calls this weaponized subordination, subordinate men employing dominant practices with their own lowness as the licence. [[Dehumanization]] — the denial of full humanity — is the result. The killings are documented; the endorsement is rare, about one in ten incels admires the men who did them. [[Echo Chamber]] — an environment that returns your own views amplified — is how the rare becomes the loud; De Vettor et al. name it as one of the main ways the red pill is taught. [[Deplatforming]] has removed the largest communities, and the migration to less moderated platforms is documented too.

**Why This Matters:** **Incel** shows what a name can do. The forums give belonging to people who find it nowhere else, which is why they are so hard to leave, and a UK survey of 561 incels ([[Secondary-Incel|Whittaker, Costello and Thomas (2024)]]) found that mental health and ideology make each other worse over time. Some do leave. r/IncelExit exists for exactly that, and those who look back tend to name the forums as what kept them there, and what made it worse.
