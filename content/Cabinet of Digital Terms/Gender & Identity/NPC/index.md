***They walk the same route every day, say the same things, react the same way. The word borrowed from gaming to describe people.***

**Literal meaning:** **NPC**, short for *non-player character*, is a game character run by the computer: it repeats scripted lines and has no will of its own. Applied to people, it means someone who goes through the motions without a thought of their own. The metaphor brings the logic of the game with it. In a game an NPC exists for the player, to give directions or stand in the way, and can be ignored without consequence. Calling someone an NPC says two things: that they do not think, and that the speaker is the one playing. [[Main Character Energy]], seeing yourself as the lead and everyone else as supporting cast, describes the same scene from the player's side.

**Origin:** The insult spread from 4chan, and by the autumn of 2018 it had become a right-wing meme for people with mainstream or progressive views, with a grey, expressionless cartoon face. That October, weeks before the US midterm elections, Twitter removed more than a thousand accounts role-playing as NPCs, citing disinformation ([[Gallagher-NPC-2023|Gallagher & Topinka, 2023]]). In 2023 the word gained a second life on TikTok, where streamers such as Pinkydoll play NPCs on purpose, repeating *"ice cream so good"* for every virtual gift bought with real money ([[Secondary-NPC|Yang, 2023]]).

> A gaming term for a character without autonomous will, applied to real people to deny their inner life — dehumanisation borrowed from entertainment.

**The Appeal:** The label explains why others hold views you cannot follow: they are not reasoning, they are running a program. That spares you the argument and leaves you as the one who sees. It also names something real: the conversation that runs on borrowed lines. [[Gallagher-NPC-2023|Gallagher and Topinka (2023)]] note that besides carrying "antidemocratic bigotry", the meme offers a vocabulary for what platforms do to the way people talk.

**The Friction:** [[Dehumanization]] — the denial of full humanity to a person or group — is the mechanism, in a lighter key than *subhuman*: the target has no inner life, so dismissal replaces engagement. [[Red Pill]] supplies the worldview, a few who see and the rest who are programmed, and [[Sigma Male]] the self-image of the one conscious observer. The streaming version turns the insult around without undoing it: the role is now chosen and performed for an [[Creator Economy|income]], and the viewers decide what the character does. [[Ogden-NPC-2026|Ogden (2026)]], writing on ASMR NPC streams, complicates even that: viewer and streamer are both caught in loops the platform sets. [[Parasocial Relationship]], the one-sided bond an audience feels with a media figure, is what the gifts buy: a person who responds, on cue, to you.

**Why This Matters:** **NPC** makes visible how gaming vocabulary can carry dehumanising logic into political talk without the alarm that more explicit language would raise.
