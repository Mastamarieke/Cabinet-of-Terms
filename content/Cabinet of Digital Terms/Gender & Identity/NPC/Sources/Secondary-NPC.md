## 1. How TikTok creators are making money off a peculiar new genre of live streaming

**Author:** Angela Yang
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** NBC News
**URL:** https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/viral/pinkydoll-ice-cream-so-good-npc-streaming-tiktok-rcna95188

### What this source contributes

Reports (19 July 2023) on NPC streaming on TikTok: creators acting like game characters, triggered by virtual gifts bought with real money, with Pinkydoll's "ice cream so good" as the phrase that went viral. Describes viewers as "the puppeteers of real-life NPCs". Source of game card 058.

## 2. The "Great Meme War:" the Alt-Right and its Multifarious Enemies

**Author:** Maxime Dafaure
**Year:** 2020
**Type:** Academic
**Publisher:** Angles: New Perspectives on the Anglophone World
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.4000/angles.369

### What this source contributes

An essay on how the alt-right revived old far-right tenets and made them look new, largely by creating and reappropriating enemy images through irony, subversion and engineered memes, and on the real-life consequences of that rhetoric. Background for the NPC meme as one of those enemy images. (Listed in earlier versions of the entry as "Phillips (2016)"; corrected 7 October 2026.)

## 4. Selective Moral Disengagement in the Exercise of Moral Agency

**Author:** Albert Bandura
**Year:** 2002
**Type:** Academic
**Publisher:** Journal of Moral Education
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/0305724022014322

### What this source contributes

Bandura's account of the mechanisms by which people switch off moral self-restraint, including dehumanising those they harm; background for the NPC label as a light form of dehumanisation.
