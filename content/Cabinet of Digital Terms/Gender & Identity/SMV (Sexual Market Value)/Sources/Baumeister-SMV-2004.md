## Baumeister & Vohs (2004) — Sexual Economics: Sex as Female Resource for Social Exchange in Heterosexual Interactions

**Baumeister, R.F. & Vohs, K.D. (2004).** Sexual Economics: Sex as Female Resource for Social Exchange in Heterosexual Interactions. *Personality and Social Psychology Review*, 8(4), 339–363. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327957pspr0804_2

---

### What this source contributes

The authors analyse a heterosexual community as a marketplace in which men seek sex from women by offering other resources in exchange. Societies then define gender roles as if women are sellers and men buyers, and give value to female sexuality (virginity, fidelity, chastity) but not to male sexuality. The "price" of sex depends on supply and demand, competition among sellers and other market factors, and the authors read gender asymmetries in courtship, infidelity, divorce and other domains through this lens.

### Analytical function in SMV (Sexual Market Value)

The academic version of the market image, from before the manosphere. It shows where the frame comes from, and why it sounds scientific: the forums did not invent the market metaphor, they turned a contested model of social exchange into a personal score.
