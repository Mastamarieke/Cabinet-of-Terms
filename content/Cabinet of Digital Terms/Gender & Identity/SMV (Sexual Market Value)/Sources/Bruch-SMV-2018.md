## Bruch & Newman (2018) — Aspirational pursuit of mates in online dating markets

**Bruch, E.E. & Newman, M.E.J. (2018).** Aspirational pursuit of mates in online dating markets. *Science Advances*, 4(8), eaap9815. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aap9815

---

### What this source contributes

Courtship is often described as a dating market, but its structure has been hard to measure. Using data from a popular free online dating service, the authors analyse heterosexual dating markets in four large US cities. Competition for partners creates a pronounced hierarchy of desirability that correlates strongly with demographics and is consistent across cities. Men and women both pursue partners who are on average about 25% more desirable than themselves, and use different messaging strategies with partners of different desirability. The chance of a reply drops markedly as the gap between pursuer and pursued grows. Strategic behaviour can improve one's chances, though the effects are modest.
