## 1. You have a secret Tinder rating, and only the company knows.

**Author:** Slate
**Year:** 2016
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** Slate
**URL:** https://slate.com/business/2016/01/you-have-a-secret-tinder-rating-and-only-the-company-knows.html

### What this source contributes

Reports (11 January 2016), after a *Fast Company* profile, that Tinder kept an internal "Elo score" for each user, named after the chess rating system: a complex algorithmic score that does not measure attractiveness as such but ranks desirability. CEO Sean Rad: "It's not just how many people swipe right on you." Source of game card 063.

## 4. Enough is enough! Japan's movement to stop lookism

**Author:** Hikaru Uchida
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** Zenbird
**URL:** https://zenbird.media/enough-is-enough-japans-movement-to-stop-lookism/

### What this source contributes

On lookism in Japan (10 August 2023). The term began in the American fat-acceptance movement of the 1970s and entered the Sanseidō Japanese Dictionary in 2021 as ルッキズム. University beauty contests have been abolished or redesigned without appearance as a criterion, and Unilever Japan has not asked applicants for a photograph or a first name since 2020, in a country where CVs traditionally carry a photo.
