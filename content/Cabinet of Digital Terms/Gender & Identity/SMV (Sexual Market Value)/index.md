***Attractiveness, status, and income reduced to a single number. The number determines your worth.***

**Literal meaning:** **SMV**, sexual market value, is the manosphere's name for a person's worth as a partner, reduced to a score, usually from 1 to 10, that adds up looks, money, status and personality. The phrase joins the most private word, *sexual*, to the language of prices, *market value*: joined, they make a partner something you can rate, compare and trade up from.

**Origin:** The market image is older than the manosphere. The social psychologists [[Baumeister-SMV-2004|Baumeister and Vohs (2004)]] described heterosexual courtship as a marketplace in which men offer resources in exchange for sex, at a "price" set by supply and demand. Pickup-artist, red-pill and incel forums turned the image into a score anyone could hand out, and it became the shared frame behind [[Looksmaxxing]], [[Hypergamy]] and the alpha/beta hierarchy.

> A framework that reduces human attractiveness and relational worth to a single quantifiable market score — applied as if it were a neutral measurement.

**The Appeal:** **SMV** brings clarity to something confusing: why some people do better in dating than others, and what can be changed. And there is something measurable in it. [[Bruch-SMV-2018|Bruch and Newman (2018)]], analysing messages on a dating site in four US cities, found a pronounced hierarchy of desirability: men and women both write to people about 25% more desirable than themselves, and the chance of a reply drops as the gap grows.

**The Friction:** The market metaphor turns persons into positions, and apps made the number real: Tinder ranked its users with a hidden Elo score, borrowed from chess, until it said in 2019 it no longer did ([[Secondary-SMV|Dent, 2019]]). [[Looksmaxxing]] raises the score through appearance, [[Tradwife]] through domestic performance; [[Incel]] is the bottom of the market and [[Blackpill]] the conclusion that the position is fixed. [[Body Dysmorphic Disorder]] is where the score turns clinical: once worth is a number, the gap between the number and the mirror reads as a defect. Outside the forums the same ranking has a name, lookism, and it is being pushed back: New York City banned discrimination by height and weight in 2023, and in Japan, where a CV traditionally carries a photograph, Unilever stopped asking for one in 2020 ([[Secondary-SMV|Uchida, 2023]]).

**Why This Matters:** **SMV** is the shared frame of the cluster: once you see it, the other terms read as positions, strategies or responses within one market.
