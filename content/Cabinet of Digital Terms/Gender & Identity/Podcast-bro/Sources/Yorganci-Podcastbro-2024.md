## Yorganci & McMurtry (2024) — "One of Us": Examining the Authenticity and Parasocial Relationships of Stand-Up Comedian Podcast Hosts

**Yorganci, K.T.M. & McMurtry, L.G. (2024).** "One of Us": Examining the Authenticity and Parasocial Relationships of Stand-Up Comedian Podcast Hosts. *Journal of Radio & Audio Media*. https://doi.org/10.1080/19376529.2024.2432023

---

### What this source contributes

A thematic analysis of semi-structured interviews with ten podcast listeners about their favourite hosts. It identifies markers of authenticity and how they feed parasocial relationships: ordinariness, immediacy and similarity create a feeling of friendship; freedom, spontaneity, imperfection and confession create a feeling of knowing the host.

### Analytical function in Podcast-bro

The source for the Appeal: why the format feels warm and trustworthy. The same markers explain the Friction, because the trust they build is not the kind an editor checks.
