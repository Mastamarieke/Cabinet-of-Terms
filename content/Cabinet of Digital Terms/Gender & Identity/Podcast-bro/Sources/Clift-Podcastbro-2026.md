## Clift, Wilson & Stone (2026) — 'You're a warrior, right?': the manosphere, podcasts, and the strongman politics of Donald Trump in the 2024 United States Presidential election

**Clift, B.C., Wilson, C. & Stone, E.T. (2026).** 'You're a warrior, right?': the manosphere, podcasts, and the strongman politics of Donald Trump in the 2024 United States Presidential election. *International Journal of Sport Policy and Politics*. https://doi.org/10.1080/19406940.2026.2625061

---

### What this source contributes

In the run-up to the 2024 election, Donald Trump, turning away from "mainstream media", increasingly spoke through influencers and podcasters, above all sport-related podcasts and "bro influencers" with a mainly male audience, such as *UFC Unfiltered* and *The Joe Rogan Experience*. The authors offer a discursive and contextual analysis of these appearances, showing how sporting, fitness and bodybuilding subcultures that reproduce particular masculinities also came to endorse conservative and authoritarian politics, and exploring the links between gender, sport and the manosphere.

### Analytical function in Podcast-bro

The evidence that the format became a political channel: the Friction's step from engagement to elections. It also shows that the podcast-bro is not only a media type but a route by which manosphere masculinity reaches presidential politics.
