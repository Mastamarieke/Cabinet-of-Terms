## Cannito & Ferrero Camoletto (2022) — The Rules of Attraction: An Empirical Critique of Pseudoscientific Theories about Sex in the Manosphere

**Cannito, M. & Ferrero Camoletto, R. (2022).** The Rules of Attraction: An Empirical Critique of Pseudoscientific Theories about Sex in the Manosphere. *Sexes*, 3(4), 593–607. https://doi.org/10.3390/sexes3040043

---

### What this source contributes

A Bourdieusian critique of Red Pill philosophy and "LMS" theory (looks, money, status), with an analysis of their effects in Italian communities: the "Redpiller" blog and two incel forums. The theories are contradictory in themselves: they combine socio-economic and biological explanations of attraction but assume a fixed, supposedly natural view of gender. They promote misogynistic content blaming women for men's dissatisfaction, but what they actually reveal are dynamics among men: hierarchies of masculinities, and tensions over whether the "biological destiny" of the incel can be escaped.

### Analytical function in Top 1% Man

The source for the three scales of the ideal (looks, money, status) and for the Friction's turn: the top 1% is less a judgement by women than a ranking of men against men.
