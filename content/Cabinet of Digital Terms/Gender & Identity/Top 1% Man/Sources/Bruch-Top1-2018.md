## Bruch & Newman (2018) — Aspirational pursuit of mates in online dating markets

**Bruch, E.E. & Newman, M.E.J. (2018).** Aspirational pursuit of mates in online dating markets. *Science Advances*, 4(8), eaap9815. https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aap9815

---

### What this source contributes

Using data from a free online dating service in four large US cities, the authors find a pronounced hierarchy of desirability, consistent across cities. Men and women both pursue partners about 25% more desirable than themselves, and the chance of a reply drops as the gap grows. Strategy helps, but only modestly.

### Analytical function in Top 1% Man

The real slope beneath the fantasy: people do aim above themselves, but by a quarter, not to the top percentile. The same study is a primary source in [[SMV (Sexual Market Value)]].
