## Krendel (2020) — The men and women, guys and girls of the 'manosphere': A corpus-assisted discourse approach

**Krendel, A. (2020).** The men and women, guys and girls of the 'manosphere': A corpus-assisted discourse approach. *Discourse & Society*, 31(6), 607–630. https://doi.org/10.1177/0957926520939690

---

### What this source contributes

A corpus study of how the words *woman*, *girl*, *man* and *guy* are used to represent gender in an anti-feminist forum on Reddit, comparing three sub-communities of r/TheRedPill: Men's Rights, Men Going Their Own Way and Red Pill Theory. The corpus is full of bare assertions about gendered behaviour, but the masculine role is less well defined than the feminine one. Women and girls are dehumanised, sexually objectified and judged negatively; men are constructed as victims of women and of institutions, and as a result as unhappy and insecure.

### Analytical function in High Value Man

The source for the gap the ideal fills: men on these forums describe themselves as the opposite of the high value man, and the role of a man is left vague. It is the wonder in the entry's semantic landscape.
