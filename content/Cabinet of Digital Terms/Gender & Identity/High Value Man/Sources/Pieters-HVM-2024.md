## Pieters & Trottier (2024) — Anti-Feminism and Self-Improvement: Hegemonic Femininity in the Manosphere

**Pieters, S. & Trottier, D. (2024).** Anti-Feminism and Self-Improvement: Hegemonic Femininity in the Manosphere. *Social Media + Society*, 10(4). https://doi.org/10.1177/20563051241302154

---

### What this source contributes

Research on the manosphere mostly looks at men, but women take part too, for instance on the subreddit RedPillWomen. In a critical thematic analysis of 127 texts, the authors show how moderators use evolutionary psychology, neoliberalism and anti-feminism to build a postfeminist femininity that is "both subordinate to men as well as highly entrepreneurial", and how members enact it by transforming both body and mind, kept in line by peer surveillance.

### Analytical function in High Value Man

The mirror of the high value man: a woman shaped for the same market, entrepreneurial about herself and subordinate to him. It places the term in a two-sided economy of self-improvement.
