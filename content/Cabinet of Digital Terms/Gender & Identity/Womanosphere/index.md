***Know your worth. Never settle. Date up. The advice sounds like empowerment; the logic comes from the manosphere.***

**Literal meaning:** **Womanosphere** is built on *manosphere*: a sphere of online spaces, this time for women. Researchers also call it the *femosphere*. The word now names two different things. In research it means female-centred communities that answer the manosphere with its own logic; in the press since 2025 it means a conservative media world for young women. What joins them is the market: both treat partners as positions to be won.

**Origin:** The communities came up from around 2018, in the wake of #MeToo: Female Dating Strategy, "high-value woman" content, and femcels, women who call themselves involuntarily celibate. [[JohanssenKay-Womanosphere-2025|Johanssen and Kay (2025)]] describe this "femosphere" as mirroring "the deeply fatalistic, gender-essentialist, 'red-pilled' logics of the manosphere". In 2025 the word took on a second meaning, when the *Guardian* used it for a right-wing media ecosystem aimed at young women: Brett Cooper's YouTube channel, Alex Clark's "cuteservatives", Candace Owens, Allie Beth Stuckey, "telling women to be thin, fertile and Republican" ([[Secondary-Womanosphere|The Guardian, 2025]]).

> A female-facing ecosystem that applies sexual marketplace logic to women's relationships — framed as empowerment while reproducing the same hierarchical thinking.

**The Appeal:** **Womanosphere** content speaks in a register of solidarity and self-respect: know your worth, set standards, don't settle. That answers something real in dating today, and gives women a framework and a community.

**The Friction:** Both versions keep the market and change its direction. The dating version takes over [[SMV (Sexual Market Value)]] and [[Hypergamy]] as facts and turns them against men: select high-value men, use your value while it lasts. [[Kay-Womanosphere-2026|Kay (2026)]] shows how women's anger on these platforms is shaped and directed by the architecture of digital media, as the manosphere's rage is. The 2025 version turns the market back towards the home: marriage, fertility, suspicion of the pill, with [[Tradwife]] as its image. Both are carried by the same [[Recommender Systems]].

**Why This Matters:** **Womanosphere** makes visible how a framework can be taken up by those it positions as objects, and how one word can be claimed by opposite camps while the market logic underneath stays the same.
