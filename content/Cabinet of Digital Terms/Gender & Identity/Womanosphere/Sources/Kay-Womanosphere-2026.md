## Kay (2026) — A tale of two angers: The manosphere, the femosphere and the gender politics of mediated rage

**Kay, J.B. (2026).** A tale of two angers: The manosphere, the femosphere and the gender politics of mediated rage. *Feminist Theory*, 27(1), 31–49. https://doi.org/10.1177/14647001251371998

---

### What this source contributes

A conjunctural analysis of mediated women's anger after #MeToo. Kay asks what it means that the historic rise in the visibility of women's anger at sexism and abuse came at the same moment as the rise of reactionary political anger, from authoritarian populism to the "anti-gender" movement. Putting these two "tales" of anger in dialogue, she argues that a media theory of anger is needed to understand how women's anger is shaped, fomented and directed through the specific communicative architectures of digital media.

### Analytical function in Womanosphere

The source for the Friction's point about anger: the femosphere's rage is not only a mirror of the manosphere's, it is produced by the same kind of media architecture. Kay is also the researcher credited with the term *femosphere*.
