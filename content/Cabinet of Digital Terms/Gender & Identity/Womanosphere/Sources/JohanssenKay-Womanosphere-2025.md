## Johanssen & Kay (2025) — From femcels to 'femcelcore': Women's involuntary celibacy and the rise of heteronihilism

**Johanssen, J. & Kay, J.B. (2025).** From femcels to 'femcelcore': Women's involuntary celibacy and the rise of heteronihilism. *European Journal of Cultural Studies*, 28(4), 981–999. https://doi.org/10.1177/13675494241293731

---

### What this source contributes

The article follows the shifting media visibility of femcels, women who identify as involuntarily celibate: first the "original" community that emerged on Reddit in 2018, built on despairing, even nihilistic text discussion; then "femcelcore", a TikTok aesthetic of short, apparently ironic videos. The authors place the original femceldom within the "femosphere", a complex ecology of female-centred online communities that mirrors "the deeply fatalistic, gender-essentialist, 'red-pilled' logics of the manosphere", and situate femcelcore in "sad girl" culture. Both seem to reject patriarchal ideals of feminine perfection and to critique neoliberal feminism, but the authors argue that they ultimately offer a weak basis for feminist politics.

### Analytical function in Womanosphere

The research definition of the term: a female-centred ecology that answers the manosphere in its own logic. It anchors the first of the word's two meanings, in Origin, and the Friction's point that the market logic is taken over rather than refused.
