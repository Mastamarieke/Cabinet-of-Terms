## Ging (2019) — Alphas, Betas, and Incels: Theorizing the Masculinities of the Manosphere

**Ging, D. (2019).** Alphas, Betas, and Incels: Theorizing the Masculinities of the Manosphere. *Men and Masculinities*, 22(4), 638–657. https://doi.org/10.1177/1097184x17706401

### What this source contributes

Ging maps the key categories of the manosphere, a loose confederacy of groups united by Red Pill "philosophy", and theorises its masculinities. While there are continuities with older antifeminism, many of these groups complicate the usual link between hegemonic masculinity and power and dominance: they operate with tropes of victimhood, "beta masculinity" and involuntary celibacy. These hybrid masculinities, she argues, are especially well suited to amplification through the affordances of social media.

### Analytical function in Alpha Male

The source behind the twist in the Friction: the vocabulary of alphas is spoken largely from the position of the beta, as grievance. That explains why the word survives the correction of its science; it is used less to describe winners than to measure loss.
