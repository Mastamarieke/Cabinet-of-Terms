***A man on your feed explains that there are two kinds of men, and that you can learn to become the other kind. The course costs money.***

**Literal meaning:** **Alpha male** names the dominant, high-status man at the top of a hierarchy in which men compete, with the *beta* as the one who loses. Alpha and beta are the first letters of the Greek alphabet, so the words themselves make a ranking, a scale on which every man has a place; the manosphere later added more letters ([[Sigma Male]]). The metaphor brings the wolf pack with it: a group held together by fights over rank.

**Origin:** The image comes from studies of captive wolves, unrelated animals put together in enclosures, and reached a wide audience through L. David Mech's book *The Wolf* (1970). Mech's own years of watching wolves in the wild changed his mind: a wild pack is a family, and the wolves that lead it are its parents ([[Mech-AlphaMale-1999|Mech, 1999]]). He has spent decades correcting the term, and the book went out of print in 2022. By then the alpha had moved into pickup-artist and [[Manosphere]] vocabulary.

> A dominance hierarchy borrowed from wolf research — later corrected by the researcher who popularised it, but kept as identity vocabulary.

**The Appeal:** The alpha framework offers a clear map: there are winners and losers, the difference can be learned, and someone will teach it. It names something many men do feel, competition and status anxiety, and gives it a vocabulary and a route upward.

**The Friction:** The science was corrected before the ideology was built on it, and the ideology carried on: the concept works without its evidence. Andrew Tate, who told boys that a real man is rich, dominant and in charge of women, was banned from Facebook, Instagram and TikTok in 2022 ([[Secondary-AlphaMale|Miranda, 2022]]). As of September 2026 he is in custody in Miami, fighting extradition to the UK, where he faces charges including rape and human trafficking, and Romanian prosecutors have indicted him for trafficking minors; he denies all allegations ([[Secondary-AlphaMale|Chappell, 2026]]). [[Ging-AlphaMale-2019|Ging (2019)]] points to a twist: the manosphere's men speak less as alphas than as victims, from what she calls "beta masculinity" and aggrieved manhood, and social media amplifies exactly that. [[Podcast-bro]] is the format through which the hierarchy is sold, [[SMV (Sexual Market Value)]] gives it a price, and [[Incel]] is where men end up who conclude they will never rise in it. In the wild, the wolf at the head of the pack is usually a parent, and its work is care: the mother guards the pups, the father hunts for food.

**Why This Matters:** **Alpha male** makes visible how scientific language can keep legitimising a social order long after the science behind it has been corrected.
