## Ging (2019) — Alphas, Betas, and Incels: Theorizing the Masculinities of the Manosphere

**Ging, D. (2019).** Alphas, Betas, and Incels: Theorizing the Masculinities of the Manosphere. *Men and Masculinities*, 22(4), 638–657. https://doi.org/10.1177/1097184X17706401

### What this source contributes

Since Web 2.0, a particularly toxic antifeminism has spread across online networks and platforms. Ging identifies the key categories and features of the manosphere, a loose confederacy of interest groups that, despite internal conflicts, are united by Red Pill "philosophy", and which has become the dominant arena for men's rights in Western culture. She theorises its masculinities: some continue older antifeminism, but many complicate the link between hegemonic masculinity and dominance by working with victimhood, "beta masculinity" and involuntary celibacy, hybrid forms that social media is especially suited to amplify.
