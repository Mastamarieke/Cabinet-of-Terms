## Wright, Trott & Jones (2020) — 'The pussy ain't worth it, bro': assessing the discourse and structure of MGTOW

**Wright, S., Trott, V. & Jones, C. (2020).** 'The pussy ain't worth it, bro': assessing the discourse and structure of MGTOW. *Information, Communication & Society*, 23(6), 908–925. https://doi.org/10.1080/1369118x.2020.1751867

---

### What this source contributes

A study of the forum on the official MGTOW website. The authors analyse the content of 1,012 comments, using Freelon's multi-norm approach (liberal individualist, communitarian and deliberative models of communication), and the broader pattern of participation: 628,745 comments by 33,863 users. Debates on the forum mix all three communicative styles, which the authors read as a contradiction in an ideology of separation and individualism. Conversation turns mainly on two topics: women, mostly discussed in misogynistic terms, and the definition of MGTOW itself, as a collective identity and a personal journey.

### Analytical function in MGTOW

This source shows the movement from the inside, on its own platform rather than on Twitter. Its central finding, that men who say they are leaving women talk mostly about women and about who they are, is the wonder in the entry's semantic landscape and the second half of the Friction.
