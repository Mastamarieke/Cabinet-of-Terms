## 1. Sexual Violence in the 'Manosphere': Antifeminist Men's Rights Discourses on Rape

**Author:** Lise Gotell & Emily Dutton
**Year:** 2016
**Type:** Academic
**Publisher:** International Journal for Crime, Justice and Social Democracy
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.5204/ijcjsd.v5i2.310

### What this source contributes

A discourse analysis of popular men's rights websites, showing a set of linked claims: that sexual violence is gender-neutral, that feminists erase men's victimisation, that false allegations are widespread, and that rape culture is a feminist moral panic. The authors argue that men's rights activism is shifting from fathers' rights towards sexuality, and uses rape to mobilise young men's anxieties about consent.

## 2. Digital Culture, Online Misogyny, and Gender-based Violence

**Author:** Debbie Ging
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Academic (handbook chapter)
**Publisher:** The Handbook of Gender, Communication, and Women's Human Rights (Wiley)
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1002/9781119800729.ch13

### What this source contributes

An overview of the contours and origins of online misogyny, from threats and pile-ons to image-based abuse, and of its entanglement with the socio-economic situation, social media affordances and male supremacist ideologies online.

## 3. Reddit Bans 'Men Going Their Own Way' Forums for Violating Hate Speech Rule

**Author:** Cammy Pedroja
**Year:** 2021
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** Newsweek
**URL:** https://www.newsweek.com/reddit-bans-men-going-their-own-way-forums-violating-hate-speech-rules-1616379

### What this source contributes

Reports Reddit's ban of r/MGTOW and r/MGTOW2 on 3 August 2021 for violating its rule on hate, and the earlier quarantine of r/MGTOW after a Department of Homeland Security report noted that a Coast Guard officer, later sentenced to thirteen years, had visited the subreddit tens of thousands of times.
