## Jones, Trott & Wright (2019) — Sluts and soyboys: MGTOW and the production of misogynistic online harassment

**Jones, C., Trott, V. & Wright, S. (2019).** Sluts and soyboys: MGTOW and the production of misogynistic online harassment. *New Media & Society*. https://doi.org/10.1177/1461444819887141

---

### What this source contributes

A content and thematic analysis of 10,280 tweets from three of the most active MGTOW users on Twitter. The authors place MGTOW as a separatist group within the manosphere that favours individual, self-empowering action over the collective action of men's rights activists and incels. They find that the harassment these accounts produce is deeply misogynistic and polices the boundaries of a heterosexual, hegemonic masculinity. The violence is not extreme in form; its force lies in the appeal to rational thinking, which makes the misogyny read as common sense.

### Analytical function in MGTOW

This is the entry's evidence for how the movement presents itself and what it does. It replaces an earlier, unsourced claim about radicalisation with a measured finding: the harm lies less in extremity than in the tone of reason, which is also why the movement's appeal to risk management can sound plausible.
