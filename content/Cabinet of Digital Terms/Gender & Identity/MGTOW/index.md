***He withdrew from relationships with women entirely. He posts about it every day.***

**Literal meaning:** **MGTOW** — Men Going Their Own Way — names men who withdraw from romantic and sexual relationships with women, and present that withdrawal as a rational answer to a legal, social and sexual order they see as rigged against men. *Going their own way* sounds like a man on his own road, needing nobody.

**Origin:** The movement took shape online in the mid-2000s, out of men's rights activism but separatist: instead of changing laws or norms, it advises leaving. Its range runs from men who simply prefer to live alone to open hatred of women. Reddit put r/MGTOW behind a warning screen in January 2020, after reports that a Coast Guard officer, later sentenced for weapons and drug offences, had visited it tens of thousands of times, and banned it in August 2021 for promoting hate ([[Secondary-MGTOW|Pedroja (2021)]]).

> Male withdrawal from relationships with women as a principled political position — where personal choice is reframed as ideological refusal.

**The Appeal:** **MGTOW** speaks to real experiences: painful divorces, custody battles, the feeling that the rules in relationships favour women. For men who have been through that, going their own way can feel like risk management rather than resentment. And some simply prefer to be alone.

**The Friction:** [[Jones-MGTOW-2019|Jones, Trott and Wright (2019)]], analysing 10,280 tweets from the three most active MGTOW accounts on Twitter, found a steady stream of misogynist harassment: not extreme, but framed as rational, so that it reads as common sense. On the movement's own forum, [[Wright-MGTOW-2020|Wright, Trott and Jones (2020)]] found that conversation turned mainly on two subjects: what MGTOW is, and women, mostly in misogynist terms. [[Sigma Male]] gives the same withdrawal a heroic self-image; [[Blackpill]] says the system cannot change, so leaving is the only rational move. [[JOMO]] — the joy of missing out — also chooses withdrawal, but as a pleasure; **MGTOW** makes it a verdict on women. [[Deplatforming]] removed the Reddit forums; the communities moved to their own sites.

**Why This Matters:** **MGTOW** makes visible how a private choice becomes a political identity. The choice to live alone is legitimate; the forums turn it into a case against women.
