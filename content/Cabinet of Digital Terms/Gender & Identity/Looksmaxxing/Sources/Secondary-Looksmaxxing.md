## 1. Looksmaxxing is het gevaarlijke antwoord op de vraag: ben ik iets waard?

**Author:** Bregje Hofstede
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** Journalism / essay
**Publisher:** De Correspondent
**URL:** https://decorrespondent.nl/16941/looksmaxxing-is-het-gevaarlijke-antwoord-op-de-vraag-ben-ik-iets-waard/7ba9fc8a-3dd5-0c35-1a9e-07fbae12e42d

### What this source contributes

An essay framing looksmaxxing as a logical answer to what Hofstede calls metaphysical precarity: existential insecurity with no intrinsic worth to fall back on. She draws on Iris Murdoch and Albrecht Dürer for historical and philosophical context. The piece also names the political dimension, arguing that whoever can promise an insecure young man that he is worth something holds considerable power over him.

## 3. The Sovereignty of Good

**Author:** Iris Murdoch
**Year:** 1970
**Type:** Philosophical essay
**Publisher:** Routledge
**URL:** https://www.routledge.com/The-Sovereignty-of-Good/Murdoch/p/book/9780415253994

### What this source contributes

Philosophical counterweight: an argument against the dominant analytic philosophy that treats only the visible and measurable as real. Morality and intrinsic human worth are not quantifiable. The entry uses this as the frame for its Friction layer, where looksmaxxing appears as what happens once this argument loses.

## 5. Handsome at Any Cost

**Author:** Joseph Bernstein
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** The New York Times
**URL:** https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/13/style/clavicular-looksmaxxing-braden-peters.html

### What this source contributes

Profile of Braden Peters (Clavicular), a 20-year-old figurehead of the looksmax movement: a $35,000 jaw operation, 19.5-inch clavicles by his own measurement, more than $100,000 a month through Kick, and millions of followers. The NYT used the term "ascended", and the profile appeared alongside comparable pieces in Le Monde and The Guardian.

- [[Looksmaxxing]] — mainstreaming of the movement through international media, with figures that make the scale concrete

## 6. Een avond met een van de meest controversiële influencers ter wereld

**Year:** 2026

**Publisher:** NRC
**URL:** https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2026/07/08/een-avond-met-een-van-de-meest-controversiele-influencers-ter-wereld-a4932055

### What this source contributes

The reviewer watches Clavicular live for six hours on Mykonos and describes what he actually sees: not the promised Chad, but a young man rating and humiliating girls on their appearance while drinking champagne alone and being turned down himself. The piece names the near-eugenic pseudoscience of midface ratios and clavicle lengths, including the fact that being white counts toward the score. It ends with the reviewer in front of the mirror, hammer in hand.
