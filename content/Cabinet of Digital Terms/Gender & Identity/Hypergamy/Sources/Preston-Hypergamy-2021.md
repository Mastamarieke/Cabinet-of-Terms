## Preston, Halpin & Maguire (2021) — The Black Pill: New Technology and the Male Supremacy of Involuntarily Celibate Men

**Preston, K., Halpin, M. & Maguire, F. (2021).** The Black Pill: New Technology and the Male Supremacy of Involuntarily Celibate Men. *Men and Masculinities*, 24(5), 823–841. https://doi.org/10.1177/1097184x211017954

### What this source contributes

An analysis of 9,062 comments on an incel forum. Incels argue that new technologies reveal and compound the practices that leave them without partners: above all, that women's use of dating apps speeds up hypergamy, and that a few highly desirable men use the apps to partner with many women. The authors argue that this focus on technology reinforces essentialist views of gender and minimises incels' own misogyny.

### Analytical function in Hypergamy

This source shows how the manosphere uses the word: not as a description of couples, but as a law that technology makes faster. It is the evidence for the Friction's sentence on incel forums, and it connects [[Hypergamy]] to the platforms. The same study is a primary source in [[Blackpill]].
