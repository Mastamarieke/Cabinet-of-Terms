## Almås, Kotsadam, Moen & Røed (2023) — The Economics of Hypergamy

**Almås, I., Kotsadam, A., Moen, E.R. & Røed, K. (2023).** The Economics of Hypergamy. *Journal of Human Resources*, 58(1), 260–281. https://doi.org/10.3368/jhr.58.3.1219-10604r1

---

### What this source contributes

The authors define hypergamy as a tendency for husbands to rank higher in the male distribution of earnings capacity than their wives do in the female distribution. Because earnings are both a cause and an effect of partner choice, the pattern is hard to measure; they solve this by using parents' earnings rank as a measure of earnings capacity fixed before the partnership. They show that hypergamy is an important feature of partner choice today in Norway, one of the most gender-equal societies in the world, and argue that, through its effect on how households divide their work, it may explain part of the remaining gender wage gap.

### Analytical function in Hypergamy

This source carries the entry's main correction. It shows that the pattern the manosphere points to is real and measurable, and that it is a structure, not a drive: it holds even in an equal society, and it feeds itself through the division of work in the household. That is the basis for the Friction's opening line, that what is contested is not the pattern but the drive.
