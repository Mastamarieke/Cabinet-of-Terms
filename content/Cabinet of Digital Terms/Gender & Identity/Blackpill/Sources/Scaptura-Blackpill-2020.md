## Scaptura & Boyle (2020) — Masculinity Threat, "Incel" Traits, and Violent Fantasies Among Heterosexual Men in the United States

**Scaptura, M.N. & Boyle, K.M. (2020).** Masculinity Threat, "Incel" Traits, and Violent Fantasies Among Heterosexual Men in the United States. *Feminist Criminology*, 15(3), 278–298. https://doi.org/10.1177/1557085119896415

---

### What this source contributes

The study asks how masculinity threat and challenges from women translate into fantasies of mass and gender-based violence, attitudes visible among some self-described incels who blame social liberalism, feminism and sexually active men for their rejection. The authors developed a measure of "incel" traits and administered it in an online survey of heterosexual men aged 18 to 30 in the United States. As hypothesised, stress about failing to live up to norms of masculinity, and endorsement of incel traits, were associated with violent fantasies about rape and about using powerful weapons against enemies.
