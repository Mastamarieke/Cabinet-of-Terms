## Preston, Halpin & Maguire (2021) — The Black Pill: New Technology and the Male Supremacy of Involuntarily Celibate Men

**Preston, K., Halpin, M. & Maguire, F. (2021).** The Black Pill: New Technology and the Male Supremacy of Involuntarily Celibate Men. *Men and Masculinities*, 24(5), 823–841. https://doi.org/10.1177/1097184x211017954

---

### What this source contributes

A qualitative analysis, by analytic abduction, of 9,062 comments on a popular incel forum for heterosexual men. Incels argue that new technologies reveal and compound the gender practices that produce involuntary celibacy: women's use of dating apps speeds up hypergamy; highly desirable men use the apps to partner with many women; and subordinate men inflate women's egos and "sexual marketplace value" through social media. The authors argue that this focus on technology reinforces essentialist views of gender, supports male domination, dehumanises women, and minimises incels' own misogyny.

### Analytical function in Blackpill

This source shows where the blackpill finds its evidence: not in biology, which it claims, but in platforms.
