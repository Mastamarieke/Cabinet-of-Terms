## 1. Beauty and the Labor Market

**Author:** Daniel S. Hamermesh & Jeff E. Biddle
**Year:** 1994
**Type:** Academic
**Publisher:** American Economic Review
**URL:** https://www.jstor.org/stable/2117767

### What this source contributes

The classic economic study of the effect of looks on earnings: people rated as better-looking earn somewhat more than average-looking people, and plainer people somewhat less.
