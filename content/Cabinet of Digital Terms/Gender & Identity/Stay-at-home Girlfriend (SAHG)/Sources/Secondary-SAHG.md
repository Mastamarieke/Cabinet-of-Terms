## 1. Gen Z women rejecting the corporate ladder to embrace their stay-at-home-girlfriend or wife status as aspirational

**Author:** Jane Thier
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** Fortune
**URL:** https://www.fortune.com/2023/10/08/stay-at-home-wives-girlfriends-gen-z-tiktok-trend

### What this source contributes

Reports (October 2023) that #stayathomewife had 245 million views on TikTok and #stayathomegirlfriend nearly 290 million, describes the typical "get ready with me" and "day in my life" videos, and cites a video of a woman packing her boyfriend's suitcase ("my bf: i'll pay for all your trips if you pack for me", more than 7 million views) and a caption reading "I don't dream of labor. I dream of living a soft, feminine life and being a hot housewife." Source of game card 065.
