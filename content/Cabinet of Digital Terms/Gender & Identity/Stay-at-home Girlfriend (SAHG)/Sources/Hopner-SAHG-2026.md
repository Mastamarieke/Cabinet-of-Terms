## Hopner & Sykes (2026) — De-Tradding: Rejection of the Tradwife Label and Rejection of the Tradwife Life

**Hopner, V. & Sykes, S. (2026).** De-Tradding: Rejection of the Tradwife Label and Rejection of the Tradwife Life. *Psychology of Women Quarterly*, 50(2), 226–249. https://doi.org/10.1177/03616843261437418

---

### What this source contributes

Drawing on social media content from Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube and Reddit, and on interviews, features and podcasts, the authors describe "De-Tradding" in two forms. The first rejects the Tradwife label or brand while still living a traditional life, on grounds of ideology, politics or faith. The second is leaving the life itself, largely in response to domestic violence, infidelity and unequal power in the relationship. Using feminist storytelling, they trace the psychological, relational and social circumstances of identity change and disaffiliation.

### Analytical function in Stay-at-home Girlfriend (SAHG)

The other end of the arrangement the videos show: what happens when the dependency goes wrong, and how women get out. It gives the Friction's warning about economic risk a basis in women's own accounts.
