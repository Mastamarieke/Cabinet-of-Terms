## Elliott (2016) — Caring Masculinities: Theorizing an Emerging Concept

**Elliott, K. (2016).** Caring Masculinities: Theorizing an Emerging Concept. *Men and Masculinities*, 19(3), 240–259. https://doi.org/10.1177/1097184X15576203

---

### What this source contributes

A practice-based framework for "caring masculinities", a concept that has become a focus of European critical studies on men and masculinities. Elliott defines them as masculine identities that reject domination and its associated traits and embrace values of care: positive emotion, interdependence and relationality. Drawing on feminist care theory, she argues that such masculinities are a critical form of men's engagement in gender equality, with potential for lasting change.
