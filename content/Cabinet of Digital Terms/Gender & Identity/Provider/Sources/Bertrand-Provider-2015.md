## Bertrand, Kamenica & Pan (2015) — Gender Identity and Relative Income within Households

**Bertrand, M., Kamenica, E. & Pan, J. (2015).** Gender Identity and Relative Income within Households. *The Quarterly Journal of Economics*, 130(2), 571–614. https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjv001

---

### What this source contributes

The distribution of the share of household income earned by the wife shows a sharp drop just past one half, where her income would exceed her husband's. The authors argue that this is best explained by gender identity norms: an aversion to the wife earning more. The aversion also shows in marriage formation, women's labour-force participation, women's earnings, marriage satisfaction, the likelihood of divorce and the division of home production. Where a randomly chosen woman becomes more likely to out-earn a randomly chosen man, marriage rates fall.
