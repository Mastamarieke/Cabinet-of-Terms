## Diallo, Le, Holzer & Popova (2025) — Online conversations between "real men": A reflexive thematic analysis of sigma male discourse on Twitter

**Diallo, A., Le, P.H., Holzer, M. & Popova, E. (2025).** Online conversations between "real men": A reflexive thematic analysis of sigma male discourse on Twitter. *First Monday*, 30(3). https://doi.org/10.5210/fm.v30i3.13648

### What this source contributes

The Sigma Male community has become a global online counterculture spreading ideas about masculinity, rejection of contemporary Western values and a return to traditionalism. Through a reflexive thematic analysis of seven public accounts on Twitter (now X), the authors examine how members build a shared identity. On a platform with few rules against provocative speech, they create "alternative truths" that serve as escapism for men dissatisfied with their lives. The community became global because it presents personal development tailored to men, attracting them with "complete acceptance and understanding of male-only struggles".

### Analytical function in Sigma Male

A source about the sigma itself rather than the manosphere in general. It supports the Appeal (acceptance, a programme of self-improvement) and the Friction (alternative truths, escapism). It replaces Ging (2019), which now sits in the secondary bundle as background.
