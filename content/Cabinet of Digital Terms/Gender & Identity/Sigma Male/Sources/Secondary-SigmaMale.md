## 1. What Is a Sigma Male?

**Author:** editorial
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Reference / lexicographic
**Publisher:** Dictionary.com
**URL:** https://www.dictionary.com/e/slang/sigma-male/

### What this source contributes

A lexicographic explainer defining "sigma male" as the term functions in mainstream informal usage: a man who operates outside the conventional alpha/beta hierarchy, independent and self-sufficient. Not a critical or academic source, but valuable as a record of how the term is explained and standardised for a general audience.

### Related entries

- [[Sigma Male]] — direct definitional source for the literal meaning
- [[Alpha Male]] — the term is explicitly positioned here as a third category alongside alpha and beta

## 3. The Sigma Male Bible

**Year:** 2021
**Type:** Self-published book (Amazon KDP), no editing or peer review
**ISBN:** 9798766034643
**URL:** https://www.amazon.nl/SIGMA-MALE-BIBLE-Strategies-Self-Discipline/dp/B09M152YVY

### What this source contributes

A self-published book that does not analyse the sigma male concept but actively promotes and sells it as a model for self-development. No scholarly grounding. Watson has no traceable academic affiliation and the name is probably a pseudonym within a series of comparable KDP self-help titles.

### Status

Not a critical or documentary source. This is an instance of the phenomenon the entry describes, included as evidence of how the concept is commercially deployed.

## 4. Alpha and beta male (section "Sigma male")

**Author:** Wikipedia
**Year:** n.d.
**Type:** Reference
**Publisher:** Wikipedia
**URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_and_beta_male

### What this source contributes

The English Wikipedia redirects "Sigma male" to this article. Its section on the term says it first appeared in a blog post by the American writer Vox Day, went viral in 2021, had more than 46 billion views as #sigma on TikTok in 2023, has taken on an ironic and satirical meaning, and that Christian Bale's Patrick Bateman in *American Psycho* is often cited as the ideal sigma, "both through memes and unironic discussion". Consulted 7 October 2026.
