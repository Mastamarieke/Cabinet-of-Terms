## 1. Word of the Year 2023

**Author:** Oxford University Press
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Reference
**Publisher:** Oxford Languages
**URL:** https://languages.oup.com/word-of-the-year/2023/

### What this source contributes

Oxford's announcement of *rizz* as Word of the Year 2023, after more than 32,000 public votes. Defines it as "style, charm, or attractiveness; the ability to attract a romantic or sexual partner", derives it from the middle of *charisma* (like *fridge* from *refrigerator*), notes the verb *rizz up*, and dates the peak in its corpus to June 2023, when Tom Holland said "I have no rizz whatsoever, I have limited rizz."

## 2. Rizz, Oxford's word of the year, resists definition

**Author:** Bill Chappell
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** NPR
**URL:** https://www.npr.org/2023/12/04/1216950808/rizz-oxford-word-of-the-year-swiftie

### What this source contributes

Reports (4 December 2023) how the word spread from Kai Cenat's Twitch streams to TikTok, and quotes Cenat from two interviews: on the *No Jumper* podcast ("Rizz started with me and a few of my friends from back home") and on Complex's *360 with Speedy Morman* ("Rizz meant 'game.' People say it's short for charisma. But like, not to me."). Also names the other finalists, including *situationship* and *parasocial*. Source of game card 062.

## 3. Rizz: I study the history of charisma – here's why the word of the year is misunderstood

**Author:** Tom F. Wright (Reader in Rhetoric, University of Sussex)
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Academic commentary (general audience)
**Publisher:** The Conversation
**URL:** https://theconversation.com/rizz-i-study-the-history-of-charisma-heres-why-the-word-of-the-year-is-misunderstood-219673

### What this source contributes

Argues that rizz is closer to *charm* than to *charisma*. Charm comes from the Latin *carmen*, song: telling an audience what it wants to hear. Charisma, the Greek "gift of grace" that Max Weber turned into a word for leaders who move masses, is the macro to charm's micro. Wright adds that, by making charisma all about charm, rizz brings "toxic gender politics and misogyny to the surface": "rizz is a manosphere term – a way that young heterosexual men measure their own and rival men's place in the world." Published 12 December 2023.
