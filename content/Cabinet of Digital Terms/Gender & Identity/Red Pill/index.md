***A film about escaping a false reality, repurposed as a metaphor for discovering that women have too many rights.***

**Literal meaning:** In **manosphere** communities, taking the **red pill** means accepting what they present as the truth about gender relations: that feminism has rigged the social and legal system against men, that women's behaviour is governed by [[Hypergamy]] and [[SMV (Sexual Market Value)]], and that mainstream society hides this. The metaphor brings its structure with it: one moment of waking, a before and an after, and a world split into those who see and those who sleep.

**Origin:** The metaphor comes from [[The Matrix]] (1999), where the red pill wakes you from a comfortable illusion. In 2020 Lilly Wachowski, one of its two creators, both trans women, said the film's "original intention" was a trans allegory: liberation from an imposed identity ([[Secondary-RedPill|Fitzsimons (2020)]]). The manosphere took the opposite meaning. Its best-known forum, r/TheRedPill, was created and run under a pseudonym by Robert Fisher, a Republican member of the New Hampshire House of Representatives, until the *Daily Beast* unmasked him in 2017 and he resigned.

> A conversion metaphor that frames ideological adoption as truth-seeking — dividing the world into those who see clearly and those who do not.

**The Appeal:** The **red pill** offers a complete framework: it explains rejection, men's social disadvantage and women's behaviour in one go. Once you take it, everything makes sense, and the community of fellow red-pillers gives belonging and recognition.

**The Friction:** The **red pill** is the entrance to the manosphere. [[SMV (Sexual Market Value)]] is what it teaches, [[Hypergamy]] what it explains, [[Blackpill]] where it ends for those who decide the truth cannot be changed, and [[Incel]] the identity of those who decide they are at the bottom. The film supplies more than the pill: [[VanVeeren-RedPill-2026|Van Veeren, Moerking and Dryer (2026)]], analysing red-pill videos on YouTube, show how the communities use the whole "image bank" of *The Matrix* to rank men and to argue for a violent "return" to a traditional order. [[Recommender Systems]] help men find it. Removing it is harder: when Reddit quarantined r/TheRedPill in September 2018, activity on the forum roughly halved, but the share of misogynistic comments stayed the same. Many users moved to small, self-moderated platforms, watched far less closely ([[Secondary-RedPill|Copland, 2020]]). The way out exists too: [[Botto-RedPill-2023|Botto and Gottzén (2023)]], reading the stories of thirty young men on a Reddit community for former "redpillers", found that vulnerability drew them in and also helped them leave. The metaphor has travelled beyond gender, into [[Great Replacement]] and [[QAnon]] rhetoric, each time with the same structure: a hidden truth, an awakening, and a community of those who know.

**Why This Matters:** **Red pill** makes visible how a metaphor for seeing clearly becomes a framework for seeing women as adversaries and institutions as enemies.
