## Botto & Gottzén (2023) — Swallowing and spitting out the red pill: young men, vulnerability, and radicalization pathways in the manosphere

**Botto, M. & Gottzén, L. (2023).** Swallowing and spitting out the red pill: young men, vulnerability, and radicalization pathways in the manosphere. *Journal of Gender Studies*, 33(5), 596–608. https://doi.org/10.1080/09589236.2023.2260318

---

### What this source contributes

The manosphere's discourses have been mapped; how and why young men join and leave it much less so. The authors describe the Red Pill as the shared ideology of the manosphere, one that adopts essentialist notions of gender and sexuality and uses evolutionary psychology selectively to support male supremacy. Based on narratives posted on a Reddit community for former "redpillers", they analyse thirty young men's experiences of entering and exiting, and show the central role of vulnerability in both. The stories are synthesised into three phases, as paths in and out.

### Analytical function in Red Pill

The entry's other sources describe the door in; this one also describes the door out, from the men who used it. It grounds the claim that the red pill answers a real need, and adds what the entry would otherwise miss: that the same vulnerability that draws young men in is part of what makes them leave.
