**Tufekci, Z. (2019).** [How Recommendation Algorithms Run the World](https://www.wired.com/story/how-recommendation-algorithms-run-the-world/). *WIRED.* — Journalistic overview of recommendation logic and its societal consequences; accessible framing of the engagement-optimisation problem.

**Tufekci, Z. (2018).** [YouTube, the Great Radicalizer](https://www.nytimes.com/2018/03/10/opinion/sunday/youtube-politics-radical.html). *New York Times.* — Op-ed that named the radicalization pathway; widely cited as the public-facing entry point to the debate, predating the empirical studies.

**Ledwich, M. & Zaitsev, A. (2019).** [Algorithmic extremism: Examining YouTube's rabbit hole of radicalization](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/339634318_Algorithmic_extremism_Examining_YouTube's_rabbit_hole_of_radicalization). *First Monday.* — Contested empirical study arguing YouTube's algorithm actually favours mainstream and left-leaning channels over extremist ones; important as counterpoint to the radicalization-pathway consensus.

**Brady, W.J., Wills, J.A., Jost, J.T., Tucker, J.A. & Van Bavel, J.J. (2017).** [Emotion shapes the diffusion of moralized content in social networks](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1618923114). *PNAS.* — Empirical study showing moral-emotional language spreads furthest on social platforms; supports the anger/fear engagement-signal claim in the Friction section.

**Kang et al. (2025).** [A Survey of Affective Recommender Systems](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.20289v1). *arxiv.* — Systematic survey of how emotions (Ekman's basic emotions, valence-arousal, Plutchik's circumplex) are modelled as parameters in recommender systems; theoretical background for the anger/fear engagement mechanism.

**[Author TBC] (2023).** [Hope, Fear, or Anger? How Emotional Framing in a News Recommender System Influences User Engagement and Openness to Non-Preferred Content](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-4056/full5.pdf). *CEUR Workshop Proceedings.* — User study (N=150): anger and fear are high-arousal emotions that capture attention and increase engagement in news recommender systems; direct empirical support for the anger/fear mechanism.
