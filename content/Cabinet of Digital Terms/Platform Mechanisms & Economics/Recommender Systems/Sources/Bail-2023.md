Field experiment testing whether algorithmic exposure to opposing political views reduces polarization. Counterintuitive finding: exposure to opposing views through social media recommendations does not reduce polarisation — it increases it. The emotional response to disagreement is itself an engagement signal, which the algorithm reads as a reason to surface more of the same. Directly supports the claim in [[Recommender Systems]] that optimising for engagement produces polarisation, not correction.
