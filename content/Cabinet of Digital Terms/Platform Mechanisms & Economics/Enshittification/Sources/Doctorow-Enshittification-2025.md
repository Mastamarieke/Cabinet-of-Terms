## Doctorow (2025) — Enshittification: Why Everything Suddenly Got Worse and What to Do About It

**Doctorow, C. (2025). *Enshittification: Why Everything Suddenly Got Worse and What to Do About It.*** MCD / Farrar, Straus and Giroux (New York); Verso (London). ISBN 9780374619329. https://openlibrary.org/books/OL42455337W

*Why a book for a general audience is a primary source here:* it is the book in which the maker of the word worked out the term. Since 14 November 2026 the Cabinet counts such a book as primary, next to peer-reviewed research.

---

### What this source contributes

The book opens with the line *"It's not just you. The internet is getting worse, fast."* Doctorow writes that he coined the word in 2022, and that the American Dialect Society (2023) and the Macquarie Dictionary (2024) made it their word of the year. He presents enshittification as a disease with a natural history, a pathology and an epidemiology, and the second half of the book as a cure.

**The natural history:** *"First, platforms are good to their users. Then they abuse their users to make things better for their business customers. Next, they abuse those business customers to claw back all the value for themselves. Finally, they have become a giant pile of shit."* The case of Facebook carries it: in 2006 it drew users from MySpace with a promise not to spy on them and a bot that fetched their MySpace messages; network effects, switching costs and the collective action problem (the group chat that cannot agree on a film) meant that *"Facebook users can't help but take one another hostage"*; then advertisers and publishers were drawn in and squeezed in turn, until the posts of friends shrank to *"a mingy, homeopathic residue"*.

**The pathology:** companies do not decay because interest rates rose or founders left, but because four forces that once held them back fell away: competition, regulation, self-help (interoperable tools such as ad blockers or third-party ink) and the power of tech workers. In the conclusion this becomes the image of a lever marked *enshittification* in the boardroom that *"used to be stuck"*. *Twiddling*, the constant adjustment of prices, rankings and recommendations, is *"the how of enshittification"*. A chapter on rent seeking discusses Varoufakis's *Technofeudalism*.

**The cure:** antitrust, regulation (with a chapter on the EU's Digital Markets Act and Digital Services Act), a privacy law, the right to interoperate, and the power of workers.

*Read for this entry:* the introduction, Part One with the Facebook case and the start of the Amazon case, Part Two, the chapters *Twiddling*, *Rent Seeking and Technofeudalism*, *Privacy First* (opening), *The EU's Digital Markets Act and Digital Services Act*, and the conclusion. Not the whole book.

### Analytical function in Enshittification

The word and the mechanism. The book gives the maker and the dates (Origin), the opening line that names what readers suspected (The Appeal), and the four stages told through Facebook, including the hostage image (The Friction). The group chat and the friend's post that no longer reaches you in the landscape come from the same case.
