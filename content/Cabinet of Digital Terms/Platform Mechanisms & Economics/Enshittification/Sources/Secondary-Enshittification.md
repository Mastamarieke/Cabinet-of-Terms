## 2. The 'Enshittification' of TikTok

**Author:** Cory Doctorow
**Year:** 2023
**Type:** Essay
**Publisher:** Wired, 23 January
**URL:** https://www.wired.com/story/tiktok-platforms-cory-doctorow/

### What this source contributes

The essay that made the word widely known. It sets out the order in one sentence, quoted on Wikipedia: *"Here is how platforms die: first, they are good to their users; then they abuse their users to make things better for their business customers; finally, they abuse those business customers to claw back all the value for themselves. Then, they die."* Three stages and a death; in the book of 2025 these became four stages.

## 4. My McLuhan lecture on enshittification

**Author:** Cory Doctorow
**Year:** 2024
**Type:** Lecture (text)
**Publisher:** Pluralistic, 30 January; given at the Transmediale festival in Berlin
**URL:** https://pluralistic.net/2024/01/30/go-nuts-meine-kerle/

### What this source contributes

The lecture version of the argument: platforms serve users, then exploit them for business customers, then exploit those customers for shareholders, and die. Four forces once held them back (competition, regulation, self-help and worker power), and each has been worn away. His remedies restore the four, and he closes with *"We can reverse the enshittification of the internet."*

## 5. The General Theory of Enshittification

**Author:** Paul Krugman
**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Essay
**Publisher:** Substack, 24 July
**URL:** https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/the-general-theory-of-enshittification

### What this source contributes

An economist's model of the same cycle: a firm builds its customer base with low prices and then raises them, and Krugman calls this *"the natural life cycle for any industry with strong network effects"*, well beyond social media (Facebook, Amazon, Uber, Peak TV). In his model the end is stagnation rather than death.

## 7. EU hits Apple and Meta with 700 million euro fines

**Author:** The Associated Press
**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** NPR, 23 April
**URL:** https://www.npr.org/2025/04/23/nx-s1-5373560/apple-meta-eu-tech-fines

### What this source contributes

The first fines under the EU's Digital Markets Act: 500 million euros for Apple, for preventing app makers from pointing users to cheaper options outside the App Store, and 200 million euros for Meta, *"because it forced Facebook and Instagram users to choose between seeing personalized ads or paying to avoid them."* Both companies said they would appeal.

## 9. Urteil gegen Amazon: Einführung von Werbung bei Prime Video war unzulässig

**Author:** Verbraucherzentrale Bundesverband (vzbv)
**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Court ruling (press release of the claimant)
**Publisher:** vzbv, 17 December
**URL:** https://www.vzbv.de/urteile/urteil-gegen-amazon-einfuehrung-von-werbung-bei-prime-video-war-unzulaessig

### What this source contributes

The Landgericht München I ruled on 16 December 2025 (33 O 3266/24) that introducing ads into Prime Video in February 2024 was an impermissible change of contract, and that the e-mail informing customers was misleading. The ruling is not final.
