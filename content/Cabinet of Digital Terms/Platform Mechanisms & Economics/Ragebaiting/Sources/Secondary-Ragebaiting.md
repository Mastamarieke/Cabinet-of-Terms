## Secondary Sources — Ragebaiting

### Academic

**Shin, J., DeFelice, C. & Kim, S. (2025).** Emotion Sells: Rage Bait vs. Information Bait in Clickbait News Headlines on Social Media. *Digital Journalism*. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/392038337

Empirical study comparing rage-bait headlines (designed to provoke outrage) against information-bait headlines (designed to signal informational value). Finds that rage-bait headlines generate significantly higher engagement regardless of the content's underlying accuracy, and that the mechanism operates at the level of the headline itself. Corroborates the Brady (2017) and Rathje (2021) primaries by extending the pattern from social posts to professional news headline design. Cited inline in Friction as the headline-level confirmation of the engagement mechanism.

### Journalistic

**Oxford University Press (2025).** The Oxford Word of the Year 2025 is rage bait. *Oxford University Press*. https://corp.oup.com/news/the-oxford-word-of-the-year-2025-is-rage-bait/

Oxford's selection of "rage bait" as Word of the Year 2025 marks the point at which the practice became widely named and recognised outside specialist/journalistic circles. Used in Origin as the naming milestone.

---

**Heijne, B. (10 juli 2026).** Influencers maken onze emoties tot hun handelswaar. Aandacht is het doel. *NRC*. https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2026/07/10/influencers-maken-onze-emoties-tot-hun-handelswaar-aandacht-is-het-doel-a4931991

Introduces the concept of *ego-kapitalisme*: the platform creator economy in which emotions — including negative ones — are commodities, and the provocation is the product rather than what it wraps. Uses Braden Peters (Clavicular) as case study for how ridicule generates the same engagement signal as admiration. Used in Friction to scale the mechanism from individual creators to a structural business logic.

**Van de Griend, R. & Pottjewijd, W. (4 juli 2026).** De populaire Dutch Travel Maniac reist in zijn vlogs voornamelijk naar een wereld van extreemrechts gedachtegoed. *De Volkskrant*. https://www.volkskrant.nl/binnenland/de-populaire-dutch-travel-maniac-reist-in-zijn-vlogs-voornamelijk-naar-een-wereld-van-extreemrechts-gedachtegoed~b31208e1/

Documents how Dutch "decline porn" vloggers — outrage content about European urban decay — were amplified by Russian bot farms. Contains quote from Guda van Noort (UvA) on the perverse incentive structure. Used in Friction to show ragebaiting operating as a disinformation vehicle at geopolitical scale, connecting to the Wellness-to-Alt-Right Pipeline.

### OSINT / Investigative journalism

**Stichting Justice for Prosperity (2026).** *Polarisatie als verdienmodel: Hoe sociale media-netwerken geld verdienen aan woede.* Amsterdam: Justice for Prosperity. https://justiceforprosperity.org

OSINT investigation of a network of 251 Dutch-facing Facebook pages (15,526 posts, 353,913 reactions in one sampled week), 85% managed at least partly from abroad and disproportionately from Vietnam. Documents ragebaiting as a purely commercial revenue model: fabricated AI-generated outrage about migrants and politicians, a "lees verder in de reacties" link routing readers to ad-filled sites, and the most polarising posts drawing ~3× the reactions of neutral ones (extrapolated to ~4 million angry-emoji reactions a year). Notably cites the same Brady et al. (2017) finding used as this entry's primary source, plus Rathje, Van Bavel & Van der Linden (2021) on outgroup animosity and Luzsa & Mayr (2021) on the false consensus effect. Used in Friction to establish ragebaiting without an ideological or state actor behind it — outrage produced only for the ad margin.

---

**Pronk, I. (13 augustus 2026).** Twee jonge Groningers verdienen geld door zoveel mogelijk mensen boos te maken met nepnieuws. *Trouw*.

Reports on the JfP findings and localises them: the Groningse *Nueva Media Network*, run by two young people, operating at least 21 Facebook pages with more than 620,000 followers on roughly ten AI-generated posts a day. Records the operators' deflecting response ("het is nadrukkelijk niet ons doel om maatschappelijke tegenstellingen te vergroten") and Peter Burger's (Universiteit Leiden / Nieuwscheckers) framing of the work as quantifying commercially-motivated hate production. Used in Friction as the concrete Dutch node of the revenue model.

---

**Neurink, Y. (2026).** Pulpnieuwsproducenten verspreiden nepnieuws over homofobe incidenten met Rob Jetten. *Nieuwscheckers* (Universiteit Leiden). https://nieuwscheckers.nl/pulpnieuwsproducenten-verspreiden-nepnieuws-over-homofobe-incidenten-met-rob-jetten/

Factcheck documenting a Vietnamese "pulp news" page (Nieuws Vandaag NL, managed from Vietnam and Brazil) fabricating homophobic television incidents about prime minister Rob Jetten to drive traffic to ad-filled sites. Shows the fabrication end of the mechanism: AI-written articles full of factual and language errors, following a reusable template. Used in Friction as the invented-content extreme of ragebait-for-ad-revenue.

### Books

**Berger, J. (2013).** *Contagious: Why Things Catch On.* New York: Simon & Schuster.

Documents the role of emotional arousal in virality — content that triggers high arousal emotions (including anger) is shared more than content producing low arousal. Provides the popular science context for the Brady et al. (2017) academic findings. Mentioned in the original entry as co-founding research reference.
