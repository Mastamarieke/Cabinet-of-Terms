## 1. Emotion Sells: Rage Bait vs. Information Bait in Clickbait News Headlines on Social Media

**Author:** J. Shin, C. DeFelice & S. Kim
**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Academic article
**Publisher:** Digital Journalism
**URL:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/392038337

### What this source contributes

Empirical study comparing rage-bait headlines, designed to provoke outrage, against information-bait headlines, designed to signal informational value. Finds that rage-bait headlines generate significantly higher engagement regardless of the content's underlying accuracy, and that the mechanism operates at the level of the headline itself.

Corroborates the Brady (2017) and Rathje (2021) primaries by extending the pattern from social posts to professional news headline design. Cited inline in Friction as the headline-level confirmation of the engagement mechanism.

## 3. Influencers maken onze emoties tot hun handelswaar. Aandacht is het doel

**Author:** Bas Heijne
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** Journalism / essay
**Publisher:** NRC
**URL:** https://www.nrc.nl/nieuws/2026/07/10/influencers-maken-onze-emoties-tot-hun-handelswaar-aandacht-is-het-doel-a4931991

### What this source contributes

Introduces the concept of *ego-kapitalisme*: a platform creator economy in which emotions, including negative ones, are commodities, and the provocation is the product rather than what it wraps. Uses Braden Peters (Clavicular) as a case study for how ridicule generates the same engagement signal as admiration.

Used in Friction to scale the mechanism from individual creators to a structural business logic.

## 5. Polarisatie als verdienmodel: Hoe sociale media-netwerken geld verdienen aan woede

**Author:** Stichting Justice for Prosperity
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** OSINT investigation
**Publisher:** Justice for Prosperity, Amsterdam
**URL:** https://justiceforprosperity.org

### What this source contributes

OSINT investigation of a network of 251 Dutch-facing Facebook pages, covering 15,526 posts and 353,913 reactions in one sampled week, with 85% managed at least partly from abroad and disproportionately from Vietnam. Documents ragebaiting as a purely commercial revenue model: fabricated AI-generated outrage about migrants and politicians, a "lees verder in de reacties" link routing readers to ad-filled sites, and the most polarising posts drawing roughly three times the reactions of neutral ones, extrapolated to around four million angry-emoji reactions a year.

The report cites the same Brady et al. (2017) finding used as this entry's primary source, along with Rathje, Van Bavel & Van der Linden (2021) on out-group animosity and Luzsa & Mayr (2021) on the false consensus effect.

Used in Friction to establish ragebaiting with no ideological or state actor behind it, where outrage is produced only for the ad margin.

- [[Ragebaiting]] — the revenue model stripped of any ideological motive

## 6. Twee jonge Groningers verdienen geld door zoveel mogelijk mensen boos te maken met nepnieuws

**Author:** I. Pronk
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** Trouw

### What this source contributes

Reports the Justice for Prosperity findings and localises them to the Groningen-based *Nueva Media Network*, run by two young people, operating at least 21 Facebook pages with more than 620,000 followers on roughly ten AI-generated posts a day. Records the operators' deflecting response ("het is nadrukkelijk niet ons doel om maatschappelijke tegenstellingen te vergroten") and Peter Burger's framing, from Universiteit Leiden and Nieuwscheckers, of the work as quantifying commercially motivated hate production.

Used in Friction as the concrete Dutch node of the revenue model.

## 7. Pulpnieuwsproducenten verspreiden nepnieuws over homofobe incidenten met Rob Jetten

**Author:** Y. Neurink
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** Factcheck
**Publisher:** Nieuwscheckers (Universiteit Leiden)
**URL:** https://nieuwscheckers.nl/pulpnieuwsproducenten-verspreiden-nepnieuws-over-homofobe-incidenten-met-rob-jetten/

### What this source contributes

Factcheck documenting a Vietnamese pulp news page (Nieuws Vandaag NL, managed from Vietnam and Brazil) fabricating homophobic television incidents involving prime minister Rob Jetten to drive traffic to ad-filled sites. Shows the fabrication end of the mechanism: AI-written articles full of factual and language errors, following a reusable template.

Used in Friction as the invented-content extreme of ragebait-for-ad-revenue.

## 8. Contagious: Why Things Catch On

**Author:** Jonah Berger
**Year:** 2013
**Type:** Popular science book
**Publisher:** Simon & Schuster, New York

### What this source contributes

Documents the role of emotional arousal in virality: content triggering high-arousal emotions, anger among them, is shared more than content producing low arousal. Provides the popular science context for the Brady et al. (2017) findings.
