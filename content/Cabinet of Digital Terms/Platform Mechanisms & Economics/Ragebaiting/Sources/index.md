Source material used in the analysis of [[Ragebaiting]].

**Primary:**
- [[Brady-Ragebaiting-2017|Brady et al. (2017)]] — empirical evidence that moral-emotional language raises retweet rate by roughly 20% per word, with outrage outperforming every other emotional valence
- [[Rathje-Ragebaiting-2021|Rathje, Van Bavel & Van der Linden (2021)]] — hostility toward the political out-group drives engagement hardest; posts attacking an opponent are shared about twice as often

**Secondary:**
