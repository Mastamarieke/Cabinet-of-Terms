## 1. Aura farming

**Author:** Dictionary.com
**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Reference
**Publisher:** Dictionary.com, slang dictionary
**URL:** https://www.dictionary.com/e/slang/aura-farming

### What this source contributes

The slang entry (24 October 2025): aura farming is "doing things with deliberate coolness, confidence, or mystique". It explains both halves, *aura* as slang for a person's distinctive energy or presence and *farming* as a gaming term for repeating deliberate actions to gain value, says the trend "appeared on TikTok and X in early 2024", and dates its wide spread to mid-2025 and the boy on the boat. The site says itself that this is an informal summary, not a formal definition.

**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Reference (meme archive)
**Publisher:** Know Your Meme
**URL:** https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/aura-farming

### What this source contributes

The archive's entry, added on 27 January 2025 and last updated on 19 August 2026. It gives a TikTok bowling video captioned "Aura Farming" (28 January 2024) as one of the earliest uses, a reply on X on 13 September 2024 as the first use of the catchphrase, viral use on X, Instagram and TikTok from mid-September 2024, and anime fans using the term to mock characters who try to look cool. The entry contradicts itself on which post came first; the Cabinet keeps both dates.

## 4. The 'aura farming' boy danced for tradition, then the internet turned it into a trend and a trap

**Author:** Hellena Souisa
**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Journalism
**Publisher:** ABC News (Australia)
**URL:** https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-08-15/aura-farming-boat-kid-draws-global-attention-and-online-scam/105641912

### What this source contributes

Reports (15 August 2025) on Rayyan Arkan Dikha, the boy at the bow in the Pacu Jalur race in Riau. Explains his role, the *togak luan* (*togak*, upright, and *luan*, the bow), who stands at the front, urges the rowers on and dances when the boat leads. His dance was imitated "from content creators to international athletes". A Canadian food vlogger was asked for US$30,000 to make content with him, through an Instagram account (@officialboatkid) that his manager says is not his. The local government gave him a scholarship.

## 9. Aura farming with memetic communication: framing celebritism through memetic layers and the DIONS Nexus of Cyberstudies

**Author:** Shane Tilton
**Year:** 2026
**Type:** Academic (peer-reviewed journal article)
**Publisher:** Critical Studies in Media Communication
**URL:** https://doi.org/10.1080/15295036.2026.2747645

### What this source contributes

Published online 27 October 2026, behind a paywall; only the abstract has been read. A case study of how Rayyan Arkan Dikha, the *togak luan* dancer, became the global meme "The Reaper": the cultural context dissolves through "narrative flattening", and his fame "arrived involuntarily through platform-mediated extraction". Tilton argues for platforms that put consent, attribution and cultural context before engagement metrics. It is about the boy, not about the word, and it can become a primary source once the full text has been read.

## 12. Explain it to me quickly: What is aura farming, and is it cool or cringe?

**Author:** Bertin Huynh, Luca Ittimani and Alyx Gorman
**Year:** 2025
**Type:** Journalism (explainer)
**Publisher:** The Guardian
**URL:** https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2025/jun/06/explain-it-to-me-quickly-what-is-aura-farming-and-is-it-cool-or-cringe

### What this source contributes

Published 6 June 2025, a month before the boy on the boat. Aura farming is "all about cultivating the coolest version of yourself", with Timothée Chalamet as Paul Atreides as the example; *farm* comes from games such as World of Warcraft and Fortnite, "to endlessly grind at a digital task", and the famous aura farmers (Piccolo, Gon, Sangwoo) come from anime. In real life you risk being made fun of: "Where there is cool, there is also cringe." And: "If someone from Gen Alpha or Z says you're aura farming, pay attention to the tone. If it's accusatory, they're mocking you. If they're laughing, they're mocking you."
