***Logistics language applied to deportation. Said without noticing it was said.***

**The statement:** *"Like Prime, but with human beings."* Todd Lyons, acting director of US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), at the Border Security Expo in Phoenix in April 2025, on how deportation should work: =="We need to get better at treating this like a business"== ([Latin Times, 2025](https://www.latintimes.com/ice-head-wants-deportations-run-like-amazon-prime-we-need-treat-this-like-business-580403): Lyons at the expo, 8–9 April).

**The context:** The remark was about the deportation system as a whole. Nine days later ICE signed ==a contract with Palantir==[^immigrationos] to build ImmigrationOS.

[^immigrationos]: A $30 million contract, signed on 17–18 April 2025, for a platform meant to give "near real-time visibility" on people leaving the country and to make finding and removing people prioritised for deportation more efficient. [Immigration Policy Tracking Project: 'Reported: Palantir awarded $30 million to build ImmigrationOS surveillance platform for ICE'](https://immpolicytracking.org/policies/reported-palantir-awarded-30-million-to-build-immigrationos-surveillance-platform-for-ice/)

**What the sentence assumes:** Amazon Prime optimises the movement of packages from warehouse to delivery point. The optimisation is for speed, accuracy, and scale. The comparison assumes that the same optimisation logic — applied to people — is self-evidently positive: more efficient, more comprehensive, better. The sentence treats the persons being deported as the packages being delivered. The dehumanisation is not polemical — it is operational. The categorical framework was so embedded that it was offered as an explanation, not a boast.

> Logistics vocabulary applied to deportation — not as a rhetorical choice, but as an operational framework that reveals how the speaker understands the people being processed.

**The analytical work the sentence does:** It makes visible what [[Dehumanization]] — Bandura's moral disengagement — looks like when it is not extreme but mundane: not "these people are **subhuman**" but "these people are inventory." The bureaucratic register is the mechanism. [[Algorithmic Violence]] — harm produced by automated systems without individual decisions — is what a platform like ImmigrationOS makes possible at scale. [[Surveillance Capitalism]] — human behaviour as raw material — is the commercial context: **Palantir** builds the infrastructure; ICE deploys it; the language of package logistics names the logic.

**Why this sentence matters:** Once you hear "like Prime, but with human beings," every logistics metaphor applied to people becomes audible as a category choice. The language is not neutral. It encodes an institutional framework. The sentence revealed it.
