## VirtualHumans.org (n.d.)

VirtualHumans.org. (n.d.). Top virtual influencers directory. https://virtualhumans.org

Industry documentation site tracking the virtual influencer market — directory of active personas, brand partnerships, and industry developments. Useful as scale reference: hundreds of active virtual influencers are currently documented, making this an industry structure, not an experiment.

## De Coninck (2026)

De Coninck, R. (2026, July 9). Sexy AI-influencers zijn niet zo onschuldig als ze lijken. 'Dit is een vorm van manipulatie en misleiding'. *Trouw*. https://www.trouw.nl/redactie/AI_influencers/

Investigative journalism piece documenting AI-generated personas posing as military personnel, nurses, and police officers — accounts that use a uniform and sympathetic backstory to draw followers, with sexually suggestive content linking to paid platforms. Documents downstream harms: financial exploitation, disinformation, transphobic and racist content, erosion of trust. Researchers quoted describe this explicitly as manipulation and misleading behaviour. Strongest secondary source for the Friction section's dark-end deployment of the virtual influencer mechanism.

## Internet Matters (2023)

Internet Matters. (2023, October 29). Rise of the AI influencer: Advice for parents on picture-perfect online personalities. https://www.internetmatters.org/hub/news-blogs/rise-of-the-ai-influencer-advice-for-parents-on-picture-perfect-online-personalities/

Parental guidance piece on the rise of AI influencers, focusing on impact on younger audiences. Notes that "picture-perfect" virtual personas can be harder to escape than real influencers since virtual influencers can be designed to be flawless by definition. Useful for the younger audience dimension and the comparison with human influencers.

## VRT NWS (2023)

VRT NWS. (2023, August 2). Lijn tussen echt en virtueel vervaagt steeds meer. https://www.vrt.be/vrtnws/nl/2023/08/02/virtuele-influencers-artificiele-intelligentie/

Dutch-language broadcast journalism on the blurring boundary between real and virtual influencers. Documents the normalisation of CGI personas on major platforms and audience responses.

## Youngworks (2025)

Youngworks. (2025, September 9). Jongeren en... virtuele influencers. https://youngworks.nl/blog/jongeren-en-virtuele-influencers/

Dutch youth research agency on young people's awareness of and attitudes toward virtual influencers. Documents that awareness is rising but that emotional engagement with virtual personas remains comparable to engagement with human influencers for many young users.

---

## Sprout Social (2025)

Sprout Social. (2025). Virtual influencers: What brands and marketers need to know. https://sproutsocial.com/insights/virtual-influencers/

Industry guide documenting the commercial logic of virtual influencers from the brand side: control, scalability, absence of reputational risk. Useful counterpoint to the follower-side analysis — makes explicit what brands are purchasing when they invest in a virtual persona.

## The Marketing Cloud (2024)

The Marketing Cloud. (2024, August 1). Should your brand invest in virtual influencers? https://www.themarketingcloud.com/blog/virtual-influencers

Industry analysis of the virtual influencer as commercial investment. Provides the brand-side cost-benefit framing and documents which sectors have adopted virtual influencers most aggressively.
