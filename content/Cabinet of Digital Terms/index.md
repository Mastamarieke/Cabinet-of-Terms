Every term in the Cabinet lives in one cluster, and every cluster is a way of asking a different question about the same digital culture. The map above draws all twenty at once: each circle is a cluster, each point on its rim is a term, and each line is a link one entry makes to another. Point at a circle to see how far that cluster reaches. Open it full screen to read the names.

The lines that leave a circle matter more than the ones inside it. A cluster with many outgoing lines is one the rest of the Cabinet keeps needing; a cluster with few is one that has not been put to work yet.
