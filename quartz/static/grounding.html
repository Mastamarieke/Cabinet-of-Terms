How this is grounded

Each part of an entry asks its own question. The questions come from research on how terms and stories shape what we see. This work shaped the questions; its names do not appear in the text of an entry.

Literal meaning

the script

What does the term literally say, how is it built, where does it come from, and what does it mean once it is used figuratively?

Runs in the backgroundFirst what it literally says: a ghost, breadcrumbs, a red pill. Then how it is built: a compound (doom + scrolling), a portmanteau (share + parenting), an acronym (FOMO), a clipping (rizz from charisma), a borrowed image (red pill), or a word that already meant something else (gooner). Then the dictionary: where it comes from and what it meant before. Where two words merge, something comes out that was in neither Fauconnier & Turner; where an image is borrowed, it brings its logic with it Lakoff
