You, in orbits of influence

The clusters of the Cabinet arranged around you, by how close they come to your daily life. The orbits follow the psychologist Urie Bronfenbrenner: where you are (microsystem), where your places meet (meso), places you never enter that still shape your life, such as the offices where your apps are made (exo), society (macro), and change over time (chrono). On a screen the far orbits reach you directly, past parents and teachers (Navarro and Tudge, 2022). Where a cluster sits is a choice, not a measurement; the lines are the links counted on 12 October 2026.
